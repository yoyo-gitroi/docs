<Info>
**URL:** `/sales/campaigns` · `/sales/campaigns/new` · `/sales/campaigns/{id}` · `/sales/campaigns/templates` · **Reach:** Sales sidebar **Campaigns** (paper-plane icon) · **Roles:** Principal ✓ Operator ✓ (Member ✗) · **Ready when:** the h1 reads **Campaigns**, **New campaign**, the campaign name, or **Email templates**, and no `Loading…` text remains.
</Info>

A campaign sends a template to the sendable contacts of one account book from the user's own Gmail, at most 50 a day about 90 seconds apart. Replies are pulled from Gmail and labelled here. Nothing is written to HubSpot.

Principal and Operator see the same controls. The difference that matters is who launched the campaign: only the sender (the person whose Gmail sends it) gets **Pause**, **Resume**, **Check for replies** and **Cancel**. Anyone else sees `Only {name} can pause, resume or check replies — it's their Gmail.` **Campaigns** is missing from the sidebar when ABM is switched off; the URLs still exist.

## Page map

### Campaign list (`/sales/campaigns`)
- Header: **Templates** (link) and **New campaign** (primary link).
- Mailbox banner: green `Campaigns send from your Gmail: {email}` or an amber reason why it cannot send.
- Table: **Campaign** (name link; sub-line book link and template), **Status** (Draft, Sending, Paused, Completed, Cancelled), **Progress** (`{n} sent · {n} queued` and failed count, or `Not launched`), **Replies** (`{n} ({rate})`), **Launched**.

### New campaign (`/sales/campaigns/new`)
- Card **Setup**: **Campaign name**, **Account book**, **Template**.
- Card **Who will receive it** (after book and template are chosen): tiles **Contacts in book**, **Have an email**, **Verified email**, **Ready to send**; info banners; contacts table **Contact**, **Email**, **Status**.
- **Start campaign** button.
- `?book={batchId}` fixes the book and hides the book select.

### Campaign detail (`/sales/campaigns/{id}`)
- Title line: status pill, book link, `· {template} · from {email}`.
- Header actions (sender only): **Pause** or **Resume**, **Check for replies**, **Cancel**.
- Tiles **Sent**, **Replies**, **Failed**, **Queued**; progress bar (aria-label `Campaign progress`); `Replies are picked up when the mailbox is checked (last checked {date}).`
- Card `Replies · {n}`: positive first, each with a label pill, **Snooze**, **Reply**.
- Card `Recipients · {n}`: **Contact**, **Email**, **Status**, **Sent**.

### Email templates (`/sales/campaigns/templates`)
- Left: **New template** button and a list of templates (name and subject).
- Right: card **New template** or **Edit template** (with **Archive** when editing), then card **Preview**.

## Recipes

### See all campaigns
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/sales/campaigns`.
2. Read the mailbox banner and the table. Click a campaign name to open `/sales/campaigns/{id}`, or the book link to open `/sales/account-books/{batchId}?step=campaign`.

**Done when:** the table is visible. Empty: `No campaigns yet` with links **Write a template** and **Open account books**. Error: `Could not load campaigns. {error}`.

### Write an email template
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** template name, subject, message.

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/templates`. Alternative: **Templates** on `/sales/campaigns`, or **Create a template** in the campaign launcher.
2. Click **New template** (button; top of the left column). The message is prefilled with `Hi {{first_name|there}},` and `{{sender_name}}`.
3. Type **Name** (input, id `tpl-name`, placeholder `e.g. HR leader intro`).
4. Type **Subject** (input, id `tpl-subject`, placeholder `e.g. Payroll at {{company_name}}`). One line only.
5. Type **Message** (textarea, id `tpl-body`).
6. To insert a placeholder, click inside Subject or Message first, then click a chip under **Insert placeholder** (button such as `{{first_name}}`; `title` shows an example). It inserts at the last cursor position.
7. Click **Save template**.

**Done when:** toast `Saved “{name}”` and the template appears in the left list.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Red list `{{x}} is not a placeholder — did you mean {{y}}?` | Unknown placeholder | Use the suggested one |
| `There is an unclosed or malformed {{ }} placeholder.` | Broken braces | Fix the braces |
| `The subject must be a single line.` | Line break in subject | Remove it |
| Error toast | Save failed | Retry once; then report |

A placeholder without a fallback (the part after the pipe, as in `first_name|there`) must have a value, or that contact is skipped.

### Edit a template
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/templates`.
2. Click the template in the left list (button with name and subject).
3. Change **Name**, **Subject** or **Message** in the card **Edit template**.
4. Click **Save template**.

**Done when:** toast `Template saved`. Running campaigns keep the copy they started with.

### Preview a template against a book
**Risk:** `READ` (nothing is sent) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/templates` and select or write a template.
2. In the card **Preview**, choose a book (select, placeholder `Choose a book`). The preview updates 500 ms after each edit.

**Done when:** you see `To {name} <{email}> at {account}` (the address in angle brackets) and `{n} of {m} contacts in this book are ready for this template.`

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| `Pick an account book to preview against.` | No book chosen | Choose one |
| `Fix the template problems above to see a preview.` | Validation errors | Fix them |
| `This book has no contacts yet.` | No contacts | Find contacts on the book first |

### Archive a template
**Risk:** `DESTRUCTIVE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/templates` and click the template in the left list.

<Warning>**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Archive**. It acts immediately: no confirmation dialog, and Sales has no unarchive control.</Warning>

2. Click **Archive** (ghost button; header of the **Edit template** card).

**Done when:** toast `Template archived — running campaigns keep their copy` and the template leaves the list.

### Launch a campaign
**Risk:** `EXTERNAL` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** campaign name, book, template.
**Preconditions:** the book has contacts with verified emails; a template exists; the mailbox banner is green.

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/new` (or `/sales/campaigns/new?book={batchId}` to fix the book). Alternative: **New campaign** on `/sales/campaigns`, or the Campaign step of a book (`/sales/account-books/{id}?step=campaign`), where book and name are prefilled.
2. Type **Campaign name** (input, id `camp-name`, placeholder `e.g. Q4 India HR leaders`).
3. Choose **Account book** (select, placeholder `Choose a book`; absent when the book is fixed).
4. Choose **Template** (select, placeholder `Choose a template`; auto-selected if there is only one).
   - Wait for: `Checking contacts…` to be replaced by the card **Who will receive it**.
5. Read the tiles and banners. Note **Ready to send** (the number that will be emailed), `Left out: {n} already emailed by an earlier wave of this book…`, `Missing values: …`, and any amber domain-mismatch warning. Report these to Clarwiz.
6. Click **Start campaign** (button; disabled until there is a name, at least one sendable contact, and a mailbox that can send).
7. A dialog `Send {n} emails?` opens: `These go from {email} to real people. … emails already sent cannot be recalled.`

<Warning>**Approval required (EXTERNAL).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Send from my Gmail**. Real emails go from the user's Gmail to every ready contact over the next days. Show the human the name, book, template, the number from `Send {n} emails?` and the sender address. To back out, click **Not yet**.</Warning>

8. Click **Send from my Gmail** (primary button in the dialog).

**Done when:** toast `Launched — {n} emails queued` and the browser opens `/sales/campaigns/{id}` (on a book's Campaign step the step reloads instead).

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| `Name the campaign to start it.` | Name empty | Fill the name |
| **Start campaign** disabled with a name | No sendable contacts or mailbox cannot send | Read the tiles and the mailbox banner; report |
| `No contacts yet` / `This book has no contacts yet. Find people on its Contacts step first.` | No contacts | See [Find contacts](/sales/account-books#find-contacts-at-the-accounts-in-a-book) |
| No templates, link **Create a template** | No template | [Write an email template](#write-an-email-template) |
| Red banner `Could not launch` or a server message | Create or launch failed | Report; a **Draft** campaign with that name may have been left behind |

### Watch campaign progress
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/{id}`. The page refreshes every 5 seconds while the status is Sending.
2. Read the tiles **Sent**, **Replies** (`{n} positive · {rate} replied`), **Failed** (`not delivered`), **Queued**, and the pacing line.
3. In `Recipients · {n}`, statuses are Queued, Retrying, Sending, Sent, Failed, Cancelled, Replied, `Replied ×{n}`, `Snoozed to {date}`. Failed rows show red error text.

**Done when:** the status pill reads **Completed**, or you have the counts. A status reason banner appears for **Paused**.

### Pause a campaign
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal or Operator, only if they launched this campaign (the sender)

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/{id}`.
2. Click **Pause** (outline button; page header; shown while Sending).

**Done when:** the status pill reads **Paused**. Error toast: `Could not update the campaign`.

### Resume a campaign
**Risk:** `EXTERNAL` · **Roles:** Principal or Operator, only if they launched this campaign (the sender)

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/{id}`.

<Warning>**Approval required (EXTERNAL).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Resume**. Queued emails start going out from the user's Gmail again.</Warning>

2. Click **Resume** (primary button; page header; shown while Paused).

**Done when:** the status pill reads **Sending**. Error toast: `Could not update the campaign`.

### Check for replies
**Risk:** `EXTERNAL` (reads the sender's Gmail) `AI` (labels each reply) · **Roles:** Principal or Operator, only if they launched this campaign (the sender)

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/{id}`.

<Warning>**Approval required (EXTERNAL).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Check for replies**. It reads the sender's Gmail mailbox.</Warning>

2. Click **Check for replies** (outline button; page header, or inside the empty Replies card).
   - Wait for: a toast. New replies show the label **Labelling…** until the AI finishes.

**Done when:** toast `{n} new replies` (or `1 new reply`) or `No new replies`. Error: `Could not check replies`.

### Change a reply label
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/{id}` and find the reply in `Replies · {n}`.
2. Click the label pill (dropdown button, aria-label `Label: {label}. Change it`).
3. Choose **Positive**, **Not now**, **Not interested**, **Out of office** or **Other**.

**Done when:** the pill shows the new label. Error toast: `Could not change the label`.

### Snooze a contact or lift a snooze
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Preconditions:** the reply is labelled **Not now**.

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/{id}`.
2. Click **Snooze** (ghost button on the reply).
3. To undo, click `Snoozed to {date} · undo` (ghost button, `title "Lift the snooze"`).

**Done when:** toast `Snoozed for 30 days — left out of the next wave`, or `Snooze lifted`. Error: `Could not snooze`.

### Open a reply in Gmail
**Risk:** `READ` (opens Gmail in a new tab) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/{id}`.
2. Click **Reply** (link; solid on the first positive reply) on the reply. Gmail opens in a new tab on that thread.

**Done when:** the Gmail tab is open. Do not write or send anything in Gmail without separate approval (EXTERNAL). Switch back to the Ariya tab.

### Cancel a campaign
**Risk:** `DESTRUCTIVE` · **Roles:** Principal or Operator, only if they launched this campaign (the sender)

1. Open `/sales/campaigns/{id}`.

<Warning>**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Cancel**. It drops every queued email at once, with no confirmation dialog and no undo. Emails already sent cannot be recalled.</Warning>

2. Click **Cancel** (ghost button; page header; shown for Sending, Paused or Draft).

**Done when:** the status pill reads **Cancelled** and queued recipients read Cancelled. Error toast: `Could not update the campaign`.

## Waits and signals
- Campaign detail polls every 5 seconds while Sending. A book's Campaign step polls every 30 seconds.
- Sending is paced: at most 50 a day, about 90 seconds apart. Completion can take days.
- Reply labels show **Labelling…** until the AI finishes.
- Launch makes two requests (create, then launch). A failed launch can leave a **Draft** campaign.
- Template preview updates 500 ms after you stop typing. The templates page has no loading text; lists simply fill in.

## Gotchas
- **Cancel** and **Archive** act on one click with no confirmation.
- Header actions are missing for anyone other than the sender.
- **Reply** opens Gmail in a new tab.
- Placeholder chips insert where the cursor was last. Click into the field first.
- In-app help says to click **Start a campaign** on a book's step 5. On that step **Start a campaign** is a heading; the button is **Start campaign**.
