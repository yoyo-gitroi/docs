<Info>
**URL:** `/sales/account-books` · `/sales/account-books/{id}` · `/sales/account-books/{id}/accounts/{accountId}` · **Reach:** Sales sidebar **Account books** (target icon) · **Roles:** Principal ✓ Operator ✓ (Member ✗) · **Ready when:** the h1 reads **Account books**, the book title, or the company name, and no `Loading…` text remains.
</Info>

An account book is a target list that moves through five steps: **Accounts**, **Review**, **Contacts**, **Emails**, **Campaign**. The list page shows every book with its next step. The book page runs each step. The book account page holds one company's compliance research.

Principal and Operator see the same controls here. **Account books** is missing from the sidebar when ABM is switched off for the workspace; the URL still exists.

## Page map

### Book list (`/sales/account-books`)
- Header: **New account book** button.
- KPI tiles: **Needs review**, **In progress**, **Ready to send**.
- Filter bar: `Sort: …` chip, `Status: …` chip, text `Sort lives in the URL · shareable`, search `Search books`.
- One row per book: title link, meta line (`{n} ICP · {n} intent · last run {when}` or `No runs yet · created {date}`), a five-step pipeline (`ol` aria-label `Book pipeline`, links **Accounts**, **Review**, **Contacts**, **Emails**, **Campaign** with counts), and the next-step action on the right with amber `waiting since {when}`.

### Book page (`/sales/account-books/{id}`)
- Header: h1 book title; links `{n} accounts` · `{n} contacts` · `{n} emails` (and `campaign running`); buttons **Add more accounts** and **Book settings**.
- Banner when the book is not open: `This book is {status}. Research and contact searches are switched off.`
- Left: `nav` aria-label `Pipeline`, heading **Pipeline**, five step links (`aria-current="step"` on the current one). Step number: green check (aria-label `done`), spinner (aria-label `running`), or amber (waiting).
- Centre: the current step.
- Right rail: **Book** (Owner, Created, Accounts, position pill, `Waiting on you`), **Sources**, **Runs**, **Cost** (Review only), **Contact runs** and **Do not contact** (Contacts and Emails), **Sender** and **Template** (Campaign), **Activity**.

### Book account page (`/sales/account-books/{id}/accounts/{accountId}`)
Hidden from nav; reached from a company name or Compliance chip in the book's Accounts table.
- Breadcrumbs `Sales › Account books › {book title} › {company}`; h1 company name; description `{industry} · {city, state, country}`.
- **Company** card: source chip, **In HubSpot** chip, domain link, **Industry**, **HQ**, **Employees**, **Revenue**, `Intent signal: {rationale}`.
- **Compliance research** card: report tabs, **Re-run**, **Earlier reports**.

## URL parameters
- List: `kpi` = `needs_review`, `in_progress`, `ready_to_send`; `sort` = `needs_me` (default), `recent`, `name`; `status` = `open` (default), `locked`, `archived`, `all`; `q` = search.
- Book: `step` = `accounts`, `review`, `contacts`, `emails`, `campaign` (absent = the book's current position); `run` = a run id (Review); `generate` = `icp` or `intent` (opens the Add-accounts sheet); `page` (Accounts table, 10 per page). `?step=emails` shows the Contacts screen scrolled to the **Step 4 · Emails** card.

## Recipes

### Find an account book
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** book name, or what state it is in.

1. Open `/sales/account-books`, or a filtered URL such as `/sales/account-books?status=all&q={text}`.
2. Click a KPI tile to filter (**Needs review**, **In progress**, **Ready to send**). Click it again to clear.
3. Click `Status: …` (dropdown chip) and choose **all** to include locked and archived books. The default shows open books only.
4. Click `Sort: …` and choose **Needs me first**, **Most recent** or **Name**.
5. Type in the search box (placeholder `Search books`).
6. Click the book title (link) to open it at its current step, or click a step in its pipeline.

**Done when:** the book page h1 shows the book title.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| `Nothing matches` / `No {status} books.` | Filters hide it | Click **Clear filters** or set Status to **all** |
| `No account books yet` | None exist | [Create an account book](#create-an-account-book) |
| `Could not load account books. ABM account books are disabled` | ABM switched off | Stop and report |

### Create an account book
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** title; optional description.

1. Open `/sales/account-books`.
2. Click **New account book** (button; visual: top right of the header).
3. In the dialog **New account book**, type the **Title** (input, id `batch-title`, placeholder `e.g. Q4 India Retail`).
4. Optional: type **Description (optional)** (input, id `batch-description`, placeholder `Who this book targets and why.`).
5. Click **Create account book** (button; disabled until a title is entered).

**Done when:** toast `Created “{title}”` and the URL is `/sales/account-books/{id}?generate=icp&step=accounts` with the Add-accounts sheet open. Continue with [Fill a book from ICP filters](#fill-a-book-from-icp-filters) or [from an intent signal](#fill-a-book-from-an-intent-signal).

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Red box in the dialog, for example `An account book called … already exists` | Duplicate title or server error | Change the title |

### Fill a book from ICP filters
**Risk:** `WRITE` `AI` (paid Clarwiz company search, runs in the background) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** industries, locations, employee range, revenue range, how many accounts.
**Preconditions:** the book is open (not locked or archived).

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}?generate=icp&step=accounts`. Alternative: click **Add more accounts** (header link) or **Fill this book** (empty-state link).
2. In the sheet `Add accounts to {book title}`, make sure **ICP filters** is chosen (radio button in the group aria-label `Where to find accounts`).
3. **Industries**: type in the search box (aria-label `Search industries`, placeholder `Search industries…`) and tick each industry checkbox. Selected industries appear as chips; remove one with its x (aria-label `Remove {industry}`).
4. **Locations**: type a location (input, aria-label `Add a location`, placeholder `e.g. India, United States`) and press Enter or click **Add**.
5. **Employees**: fill `Employees minimum` (placeholder `Min, e.g. 100`) and `Employees maximum` (placeholder `Max, e.g. 5,000`).
6. **Annual revenue (USD)**: fill `Annual revenue (USD) minimum` and `Annual revenue (USD) maximum`.
7. Read the ledger hint under the form. `These exact filters have already returned {n} account(s). This run fetches the next {n}…` is fine. `Clarwiz has already returned everything it has for these exact filters.` blocks the run: change a filter.
8. Set **New accounts to find** (input, id `icp-count`, default 20).
9. Click **Start research** (button; visual: bottom of the sheet).

**Done when:** toast `Research started — you can leave this page`, the sheet closes, URL is `?step=accounts`, and a run card `ICP run · started {when}` appears on the Accounts step. Then [wait for the run](#wait-for-a-research-run-to-finish).

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| `Minimum employees must be a whole number.` (or Maximum employees, Minimum revenue, Maximum revenue) | Non-integer input | Enter whole numbers |
| `Minimum employees is above the maximum.` / `Minimum revenue is above the maximum.` | Range reversed | Fix the range |
| Amber `No filters set — this will pull from everything Clarwiz has.` | No filters | Add at least one filter |
| `Enter a number between 1 and {max}.` | Count out of range | Fix the count |
| `This book is not open, so research is switched off.` | Book locked or archived | Reopen it in [Book settings](#change-book-settings) |
| Toast `Could not start research` or a server message | Start failed | Report the text |

### Fill a book from an intent signal
**Risk:** `AI` (Claude web research plus a Clarwiz lookup per company) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** the buying signal; number of companies; model if specified.

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}?generate=intent&step=accounts`.
2. In the sheet, choose **Intent signal** (radio button).
3. Type in **Intent signal** (textarea, id `intent-query`, placeholder `Describe the buying signal: who is doing what, where, and how recently.`; at least 10 characters). Or click an example chip to fill it.
4. Choose **Model** (select, id `intent-model`) if Clarwiz named one.
5. Set **Companies to find** (input, id `intent-count`, default 10, 1 to 50).
6. Click **Start research**.

**Done when:** toast `Research started — you can leave this page` and a run card `Intent run · started {when}` with a progress bar and a live **Behind the scenes** trace. Then [wait for the run](#wait-for-a-research-run-to-finish).

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| `Describe the signal in a little more detail.` | Under 10 characters | Write more |
| `Enter a number between 1 and 50.` | Count out of range | Fix it |
| `A run is already generating for this book. You can start another.` | Another run is active | Fine to continue |

### Wait for a research run to finish
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Stay on `/sales/account-books/{id}?step=accounts`. The page polls every 3 seconds while a run is queued or running. A typical run takes 3 to 6 minutes.
2. Watch the run card: progress bar (aria-label `Enrichment progress`, text `Enriched {done} of {total} companies…`).
3. If you leave and come back, open `/sales/account-books/{id}?step=review` instead. The completion toast only fires if the page is open when the run ends.

**Done when:** toast `Research finished — results are waiting in Review`, or run chip **Ready for review**, or the rail shows `Waiting on you` with `{n} run(s) in Review`.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Run chip **Failed** | Run failed | See [Handle a failed run](#handle-a-failed-run) |
| `Resumed after an interruption — attempt {n} of {m}.` | Server retried | Keep waiting |

### Review a run and add accounts
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** which companies to add (default: all ticked).

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}?step=review` (the newest staged run opens) or `?run={runId}&step=review`.
2. Check the heading **Review results** and the pill **Waiting for you**.
3. Open each group (toggle button on its header) and decide:
   - **New** `{n} · ticked by default`
   - **Already in your tenant** `{n} · unticked, add them to this book too? No re-enrichment needed.`
   - **Already in this book** (read only) and **Could not enrich** (read only).
4. Tick or untick rows (checkbox, id `review-{itemId}`), or use **Select all** / **Clear all** on a group, or **Select none** in the sticky bar.
5. Click `Add {n} account(s) to this book` (primary button in the sticky bar at the bottom, `title "Add to {book}"`; disabled at 0).

**Done when:** toast `Added {n} account(s)` (optionally `· {k} already there`) and URL changes to `?step=accounts`. The run card then reads **Added to book** or **Nothing new to add**.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Toast `Could not add accounts` or a server message | Commit failed | Retry once; then report |
| `Nothing to review` with **Add accounts** | No staged run | Fill the book first |

### Handle a failed run
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

There is no retry button on a failed run. Read the red banner on the Review step, then start a new run if the cause is fixable.

| Banner text | Do this |
|---|---|
| `Clarwiz could not complete this search ({reason}). Check the filters and try again.` | Change filters; start a new ICP run |
| `Clarwiz is not configured for this workspace. An administrator needs to set the webhook URL and token.` | Stop; report to an admin |
| `Clarwiz could not be reached. Try again in a few minutes.` | Wait; start a new run |
| `Intent research is not set up: the Anthropic API key on the server is missing or invalid. An administrator needs to update ANTHROPIC_API_KEY.` | Stop; report |
| `The request to Claude failed. Try again in a few minutes.` | Wait; start a new run |
| `The model declined this intent query. Rephrase it and try again.` | Rephrase the intent signal |
| `The model ran out of room before finishing. Narrow the query or ask for fewer accounts.` | Narrow it or lower the count |
| `The model did not return a usable account list. Try a more specific intent query.` | Make it more specific |
| `The run was interrupted and ran out of retries. Start it again.` | Start a new run |
| `The run failed without a recorded reason.` | Start a new run once; then report |

### Run compliance research on several accounts
**Risk:** `AI` (Claude web research; the dialog shows the cost estimate) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** which companies; model.

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}?step=accounts`.
2. Tick rows (checkbox, aria-label `Select {name}`), or tick the header box (aria-label `Select the accounts on this page`). Maximum 25 selected; further checkboxes disable.
3. Click `Research {n}` (button; shows **Research selected** before rows are ticked; visual: top right of the Accounts card).
4. In the dialog `Research {n} company` / `companies`, choose **Model** (select, id `compliance-model`). Read the cost line (`Rough estimate: about $1.50 to $4 per company` or `about $3 to $8 per company`, `Each run takes several minutes.`) and tell Clarwiz the estimate.
5. Click **Start research**.

**Done when:** toast `Compliance research: {n} queued · {n} already researched · {n} already running · {n} skipped (no usable domain)`. The **Compliance** column chips move through **Queued** and **Researching** to a date (refreshes every 10 seconds).

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Toast `Could not start research` or a server message | Start failed | Report |
| Chip **Failed** | Research failed for that company | Open the company and [re-run](#run-compliance-research-for-one-company) |

### Remove an account from a book
**Risk:** `DESTRUCTIVE` (book membership only) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** company name.

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}?step=accounts` and find the row (10 per page; use **Next** if needed).

<Warning>**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE).** Stop here and get approval before removing. The company leaves this book. It stays in HubSpot and in any other book.</Warning>

2. Click **Remove** (ghost button with a trash icon, aria-label `Remove {name} from this book`; visual: last column of the row).
3. In the popover `Remove {name}?` (text `It leaves this book only. It stays in HubSpot and in any other book that uses it.`), click **Remove**.

**Done when:** toast `Removed {name} from this book`. Error toast: `Could not remove account`.

### Find contacts at the accounts in a book
**Risk:** `WRITE` (paid Clarwiz people search, runs in the background) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** job titles; seniority levels.
**Preconditions:** the book is open and has accounts.

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}?step=contacts`.
2. In the card **Find contacts** (pill **Step 3**), edit **Job titles** (input, id `people-titles`, placeholder `e.g. VP Operations, Head of People, CHRO`; separate with commas, semicolons or new lines; max 25).
3. Toggle **Seniority** buttons (group aria-label `Seniority`, each `aria-pressed`): Founder, Owner, Partner, C-suite, VP, Director, Head, Manager, Senior, Mid-level, Entry, Intern. Defaults: C-suite, VP, Director.
4. Click `Find people at {n} account(s)` (button).
   - Wait for: the run line `Searching…`. The page refreshes every 3 seconds while it works.

**Done when:** the run line shows its summary, the rail shows `People search · done {when}`, and the card `Contacts · {n}` lists people.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Button disabled | Book not open, a people run is active, or 0 accounts | Wait for the active run, or fix the book |
| `Clarwiz is not configured for people search or email finding.` | Not set up | Stop; report |
| `Clarwiz could not be reached. Try again in a few minutes.` | Outage | Wait; retry |
| `The run was interrupted and ran out of retries. Start it again.` | Failed | Start again |
| Toast `Could not start` or a server message | Start failed | Report |

### Find emails for contacts
**Risk:** `WRITE` (paid Clarwiz email finder) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}?step=emails` (scrolls to the card **Step 4 · Emails**, id `emails`).
2. Read `{n} of {m} contacts have an email. {k} still need one.`
3. Click `Find emails for {n} contact(s)` (button; disabled with 0 candidates, a closed book, or an active run).
   - Wait for: `Looking up emails — this can take a minute…`; the page refreshes every 3 seconds.

**Done when:** the status pill reads **Done** and the progress bar (aria-label `Contacts with an email`) and `{pct} percent of this book is reachable today.` update. Failure: pill **Failed** with the same run-line messages as people search.

### Filter and export contacts
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}?step=contacts`.
2. In the card `Contacts · {n}`, use `Seniority: …`, `Email: …` (any, Verified, Guessed, Not found), **Sendable only** (toggle, `aria-pressed`), and the search box (aria-label `Search`).
3. If an amber banner says `{n} emails are at a different domain than the company. Check before sending.`, click **Show** to filter to those contacts (each has an amber **Domain mismatch** pill).
4. Click `Show all {n}` at the bottom to see beyond 25 rows.
5. Click **Export** (button; card header) to download `{book-title}-contacts.csv`.

**Done when:** the list is filtered or the CSV downloaded. Status values: **Verified**, **Entered by hand**, **Guessed**, **Not found**, **Not looked up yet**, **No email**, **Do not contact**.

### Edit a contact or mark do not contact
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** contact name; fields to change; reason if flagging.

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}?step=contacts` and find the contact.
2. Click the pencil (icon button, aria-label `Edit {name}`; visual: right end of the row).
3. In the dialog `Edit {full name}`, change **First name** (id `c-first`), **Last name** (id `c-last`), **Job title** (id `c-title`), **Email** (id `c-email`) as needed.
4. To stop all emails to this person, tick **Do not contact** (checkbox, id `c-dnc`) and fill **Why (kept on the contact)** (id `c-dnc-reason`, max 200).
5. Click **Save**.

**Done when:** toast `Contact updated`. Your edits are kept; later searches do not overwrite them. A flagged contact is never sendable.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Red box such as `A contact needs a name` or `… is not an email address` | Validation | Fix the field |

### Start a campaign wave from the book
**Risk:** `EXTERNAL` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Preconditions:** the book has sendable contacts; an email template exists; the user's Gmail can send.

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}?step=campaign`.
2. If a campaign already exists and is **Completed** or **Cancelled**, click **Start the next wave** (button in the card **What this step is**). Otherwise the **Start a campaign** card is already showing (that text is a heading, not a button).
3. Follow [Launch a campaign](/sales/campaigns#launch-a-campaign) from step 3. The book is fixed and the name is prefilled `{book} · wave {n}`. Only sendable contacts are used, never anyone an earlier wave emailed.

**Done when:** toast `Launched — {n} emails queued` and the step reloads to `Campaign · {name}`.

### Watch a running wave
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}?step=campaign`. The step refreshes every 30 seconds while sending.
2. Read the status link `{status} · {sent} of {total}`, the progress bar (aria-label `Wave progress`), tiles **Sent**, **Replies**, **Failed**, **Queued**, and the **Replies** card.
3. Click **Open in Campaigns**, a tile, or **View all replies** to manage the campaign at `/sales/campaigns/{id}`.

**Done when:** you have the counts. Pause, resume, cancel and reply handling are on [Campaigns](/sales/campaigns).

### Change book settings
**Risk:** `WRITE`; `DESTRUCTIVE` when archiving · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** new title, description or status.

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}`.
2. Click **Book settings** (button; page header).
3. In the dialog **Book settings**, edit **Title** (id `book-title`) and **Description** (id `book-description`).
4. To change status, choose **Open**, **Locked** or **Archived** (radio group aria-label `Status`). Locked and archived books keep their data but take no new research.

<Warning>**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE).** Stop here and get approval before saving a change to **Archived**. Archived books are hidden from the default list and take no new research. You can set it back to **Open** in this dialog.</Warning>

5. Click **Save** (disabled with an empty title).

**Done when:** toast `Book saved`. Error: red banner with the server message or `Could not save`.

There is no delete-book action anywhere in the app.

### Run compliance research for one company
**Risk:** `AI` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** company; model.

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}/accounts/{accountId}`. Alternative: click the company name or its Compliance chip in the book's Accounts table.
2. If no report exists, click **Run compliance research** (button in the empty state; disabled until the model list loads). If a report exists, click **Re-run** (outline button).
3. In the dialog (`Run compliance research` or `Re-run compliance research?`), choose **Model** and click **Start research** or **Re-run research**.
   - Wait for: `Researching {name} with {model}. This usually takes several minutes; you can leave this page and come back.` with a live **Behind the scenes** trace. The page polls every 3 seconds.

**Done when:** toast `Compliance research ready` and the report tabs appear.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Toast `Already researched on {date}` | A report exists | Use it, or choose **Re-run** |
| Toast `Research for this company is already running` | In progress | Wait |
| Toast `Compliance research failed` / banner `The last research attempt ({date}) failed…` | Run failed | Re-run once; then report |
| Toast `Could not start research` | Start failed | Report |

A re-run replaces the report for every page that shows that domain, including HR Roadmap. The old report stays under **Earlier reports**.

### Read a compliance report
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/sales/account-books/{id}/accounts/{accountId}`.
2. Click a report tab (role tab): **Profile**, `Compliance ({n})` (default), `What's new ({n})`, **Top risks**, **Value map**, **Call guide**, **Flags & unknowns**, `Sources ({n})`.
3. For an older version, open **Earlier reports** (select; shown only with more than one report) and pick `{date} · {model}`.

**Done when:** the tab content is visible. The card description shows `Researched on {date} with {model}`.

## Waits and signals
- Book research runs: poll every 3 seconds; 3 to 6 minutes. Done: **Ready for review**. Failed: **Failed** plus a red banner on Review.
- Compliance research: several minutes. Book Compliance column polls every 10 seconds; the company page every 3 seconds while running.
- People search and email finder: poll every 3 seconds while a run is active.
- Campaign step: polls every 30 seconds while sending.
- Run status labels: **Queued**, **Generating**, **Ready for review**, **Added to book**, **Failed**, **Cancelled**.
- Loading text: `Loading account books…`, `Loading account book…`, `Loading contacts…`, `Loading campaign…`, `Loading options…`, `Loading…`.

## Gotchas
- The list defaults to **open** books. Set Status to **all** to see locked or archived ones.
- The "Research finished" toast only appears if the book page is open when the run ends. Check the Review step after coming back.
- The Add-accounts sheet is driven by the URL. Back and Forward can reopen it; closing it removes `generate` from the URL.
- Remove is a small popover next to the button, not a modal.
- Bulk compliance research is capped at 25 selected accounts.
- Failed runs have no retry button. Start a new run.
- `?step=contacts` and `?step=emails` show the same screen.
- In-app help says `"This book is locked"`; the app shows `This book is {status}. Research and contact searches are switched off.` on the page and `This book is not open, so research is switched off.` in the sheet.
- In-app help calls the button **Research selected**; once rows are ticked it reads `Research {n}`.
- In-app help lists next-step buttons as Review, Find contacts, Find emails, Start campaign. The list can also show `Fill this book`, `Research running` (plain text), `Finding contacts`, `Finding emails`, `{sent} sent · {n} replies` and `Archived` (plain text).
