<Info>

**URL:** `/approvals` and `/compliance` · **Reach:** top tabs **Approvals** and **Compliance** (the tab bar shows on `/requests`, `/approvals`, `/compliance`, not on `/`) · **Roles:** Principal ✓ Operator ✓ (no role gating in either page; HR Intel links on `/compliance` are Principal and Admin only) · **Ready when:** `/approvals`: h1 **Approvals** and the left card **Contact change review** shows rows or an empty text. `/compliance`: h1 **Compliance** and the card title `Compliance Items ({n})` with the table loaded.

</Info>

Two separate review pages. `/approvals` is the **contact-change** review queue (not the Approvals card on Your day). `/compliance` tracks client compliance obligations. Neither page sends email or updates HubSpot.

<Note>Unverified: server permission per role for contact-change decisions. The page calls are fixed to the tenant "ariya".</Note>

## Page map

**Approvals — `/approvals`**
- Header: h1 **Approvals**, button **Refresh** (top right).
- Left card **Contact change review**: status buttons **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Applied**, **Failed**, then request rows (buttons).
- A drag handle between the panes (aria-label "Resize approvals list").
- Right card **Request detail**: Requester, Client id, Updated, Reason, **Field changes** table (Field / Current / Proposed), **Audit events**, then:
  - for a pending request: **Decision note** box with **Reject** and **Approve**;
  - for an approved request: **Apply approved change** box with **Apply Local Change**.

**Compliance — `/compliance`**
- Header: h1 **Compliance**, button **Sync outstanding items** (top right).
- Optional "HR Intel source handoff" box (only when the URL has `source=hr-intel`).
- Info card "Compliance operator boundary".
- Stat buttons: **Overdue**, **Due 7 Days**, **Due 14 Days**, **Due 30 Days**, **Completed**.
- Card `Compliance Items ({n})`: filter bar (search "Search rules, clients...", selects **Client**, **Rule**, **Due Date**, **Status**, **Category**, filter chips, **Clear all**) and a table with columns **Rule**, **Client**, **Category**, **Source**, **Due Date**, **Days**, **Status**, actions (**✓ Mark done**, **Waive**).
- Footer `{n} clients evaluated.`

## Recipes

### Review a contact change request
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** request id (optional), or the contact or client name

1. Open `/approvals?request={id}` to preselect a request, or open `/approvals`.
   - Wait for: "Loading approval queue..." to go.
2. Click a status button (**Pending** is the usual one) at the top of **Contact change review**.
3. Click the request row (button; visual: left list).
   - Wait for: "Loading request detail..." to be replaced by the detail.
4. Read **Reason**, the **Field changes** table (Field / Current / Proposed) and **Audit events**.

**Done when:** the right card shows the request detail.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| `No {status} contact requests.` / "New requests will appear here." | Nothing in this status | Try another status button or report. |
| "Choose a request to review." / "No request selected." | Nothing selected | Click a row. |

### Approve a contact change
**Risk:** `WRITE` (Ariya only) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** which request; optional approval note

1. Open the pending request (see [Review a contact change request](#review-a-contact-change-request)).
2. Optional: type a note in the **Decision note** box (textarea, placeholder "Add approval context or rejection reason...").
3. Click **Approve** (button; shows "Approving..."; visual: bottom right of the detail card).

**Done when:** toast "Contact change approved." and the request leaves the **Pending** list. The change is not yet applied to contacts; see [Apply an approved contact change](#apply-an-approved-contact-change).

### Reject a contact change
**Risk:** `DESTRUCTIVE` (no undo on this page) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** which request; the rejection reason (required)

1. Open the pending request.
2. Type the reason in the **Decision note** box (textarea, placeholder "Add approval context or rejection reason..."). Required.

<Warning>**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Reject**. This page has no control to undo a rejection.</Warning>

3. Click **Reject** (red button; shows "Rejecting...").

**Done when:** toast "Contact change rejected." and the request leaves **Pending**.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| "A rejection reason is required" | Note is empty | Type the reason and click **Reject** again. |
| Red alert / toast with another message | Server refused | Report the message. |

### Apply an approved contact change
**Risk:** `WRITE` (Ariya contacts only; HubSpot is not touched) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/approvals` and click **Approved** (status button).
2. Click the request row.
3. Optional: type in the box (textarea, placeholder "Optional apply note...") under **Apply approved change**.
4. Click **Apply Local Change** (button; shows "Applying...").

**Done when:** toast "Contact change applied locally." (or "Contact change was already applied.") and the list switches to **Applied**.

### Reload the approval queue
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Click **Refresh** (button; visual: top right of `/approvals`).

**Done when:** the list re-renders.

### Find compliance items
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** client slug or name, due window, status (any of these)

1. Open `/compliance?client={slug}` to pre-filter one client, or open `/compliance`.
   - Wait for: the table skeleton to go.
2. To filter by due date, use the **Due Date** select ("All Dates", "Overdue", "Due Today", "Due Next 7 Days", "Due Next 14 Days", "Due Next 30 Days", "Due This Month"). For overdue items choose **Overdue** here, not the stat button.
3. Optional: **Status** select ("All Statuses", "Pending", "Completed", "Waived"), **Client** ("All Clients"), **Rule** ("All Rules"), **Category** ("All Categories"), or type in the search box (placeholder "Search rules, clients...").
4. Optional: click a stat button **Due 7 Days**, **Due 14 Days**, **Due 30 Days** or **Completed** to set filters in one click.
5. To remove one filter, click the × on its chip (`{Filter}: {value}`; the × has no label). To remove all, click **Clear all**.

**Done when:** the table shows the matching rows.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| "No compliance items yet. Sync outstanding items to evaluate client obligations." | Nothing tracked yet | See [Sync outstanding compliance items](#sync-outstanding-compliance-items). |
| "No items match the current filters." | Filters too narrow | Click **Clear all**. |
| Clicking **Overdue** (stat) shows every item | Known bug: that button sets a status the filter ignores | Use **Due Date** → **Overdue**. |

### Sync outstanding compliance items
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Open `/compliance`.
2. Click **Sync outstanding items** (button; shows "Syncing…"; visual: top right).
   - Wait for: a toast; this re-evaluates every client and can take a while.

**Done when:** toast `Synced {n} clients — {k} new item(s) created ({t} tracked total).` or `Synced {n} clients — already up to date ({t} tracked).`

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Toast "Sync failed" | Server error | Retry once, then report. |

### Sync one client from an HR Intel handoff
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator (the page is usually reached from HR Intel, which only Principal and Admin can open)

1. The page is open with an "HR Intel source handoff" box (URL has `source=hr-intel` and `client={slug}`).
2. Click **Sync client obligations** (button in the box; shows "Syncing...").

**Done when:** a sync toast appears and the table updates for that client.

Other links in the box: **Back to client** (`/clients/{slug}`), **Back to HR Intel** (Principal and Admin only), **Manage trusted sources** (`/admin/trusted-sources`).

### Mark a compliance item complete
**Risk:** `WRITE` (no client communication) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** rule and client; optional evidence notes

1. Find the row (see [Find compliance items](#find-compliance-items)).
2. Click **✓ Mark done** (button on the row; only on open rows).
   - Dialog "Mark item complete" opens; its description is the rule.
3. Optional: type in **Evidence notes** (placeholder `e.g. "Training completed for 12 employees"`).
4. Click **Mark complete** (button; shows "Processing..."). **Cancel** backs out.

**Done when:** toast "Marked complete" and the row status changes.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Toast "Failed to mark complete" | Server error | Retry once, then report. |

### Waive a compliance item
**Risk:** `DESTRUCTIVE` (no undo on this page; no client communication) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** rule and client; the reason (required)

1. Find the row.
2. Click **Waive** (button on the row; only on open rows).
   - Dialog "Waive compliance item" opens.
3. Type the reason in **Reason** (placeholder "Why is this being waived?"). Required.

<Warning>**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Waive**. The page has no control to undo a waiver.</Warning>

4. Click **Waive** (red button in the dialog; shows "Processing..."). **Cancel** backs out.

**Done when:** toast "Item waived".

### Open the source law for an item
**Risk:** `READ` (opens a new tab) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. In the row's **Source** column click the law name or **Open source** (link).
   - It opens in a **new tab**. Switch back to the Ariya tab when done.

## Waits and signals
- `/approvals`: "Loading approval queue..." (list), "Loading request detail..." (detail). Buttons show "Approving...", "Rejecting...", "Applying...".
- `/compliance`: table skeleton while loading; "Syncing…" on sync; dialogs show "Processing..." while saving.
- Toasts: "Contact change approved.", "Contact change rejected.", "Contact change applied locally.", "Contact change was already applied.", "Marked complete", "Item waived", sync toasts above.

## Gotchas
- The **Overdue** stat button on `/compliance` shows all items. Use **Due Date** → **Overdue**.
- Source links open a new tab.
- The **Approvals** tab is contact changes only. Memory and document approvals are on [Your day](/command-center/your-day#approve-and-deliver-a-document).
- **Apply Local Change** updates Ariya contacts only; HubSpot is not touched.
- The compliance research dialog and report used under Sales are not on this page.
