<Info>
**URL:** `/people`, `/people/{id}` (also `/contacts`, `/contacts/{id}`, see warning) · **Reach:** Workspace sub-nav **Contacts**, or client **People & Roles** tab → **People Directory** · **Roles:** Principal ✓ Operator ✓ (read; saving Gmail messages returns 403 for Operator) · **Ready when:** H1 **Contacts** with description "Manage current-client contacts, role coverage, and person profiles." and the table loaded.
</Info>

The cross-client contact directory. It is read-only: contact changes are made from a client's **People & Roles** tab as change requests ([People and Roles tab](/clients/client-tabs-people-hr#people-and-roles-tab)).

<Warning>
The sub-nav item **Contacts** opens `/people`, not `/contacts`. The separate `/contacts` pages (HubSpot contact browser) call API routes that are not mounted on the server, so they show empty lists, "Contact not found", and failed submits. Use `/people` and the client **People & Roles** tab instead. See [HubSpot contacts pages](#hubspot-contacts-pages-not-working).
</Warning>

## Page map: `/people`
- H1 **Contacts**, description as above.
- Tiles: **Current contacts**, **Client-linked** (both switch scope to current clients), **Role gaps** (link to `/clients?view=profile-workspace&profile_gap=primary_contact`), **Profile-ready**, **Missing method**.
- Card **Role coverage queue** with links **Primary contact** and **HR/Payroll contact** (open the Profile Workspace category).
- Scope buttons **Current clients** and **All contacts**; text "Open a row to review the person profile and contact history."
- Table: **Person**, **Clients** (badges link to `/clients/{slug}?tab=people`, `+N`, or **Unassigned**), **Client roles** (or **Needs role**), **Contact method** (email link, phone, or **Missing contact method**), **Profile status** (**Ready** or a list such as "Missing client, role, contact method").

## Recipes

### Find a person
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** all
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** name, email, phone, title or role.

1. Open `/people`.
   - Wait for: tiles and table rows.
2. Click **All contacts** (scope button) if the person may not belong to a current client; **Current clients** is the narrower scope.
3. Type in the search box (input, placeholder "Search people, emails, roles, or clients..."). It searches name, email, phone, title and roles. It does not search client names, despite the placeholder.
4. Read the row, or click it to open the profile.

**Done when:** the person's row is visible.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| "No people records yet." | No match, or the load failed (errors are not shown) | Try **All contacts**, a shorter search; reload once |
| Need people at one client | Client names are not searchable here | Open `/clients/{slug}?tab=people` instead |

### Open a person profile
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** all

1. On `/people`, click the person's row. It does a full page load to `/people/{id}?from=/people`.
   - Wait for: "Loading person profile..." to be replaced by H1 = person name and the line `{title or Person profile} · {n} client relationship(s)`.
2. Read **People Setup** (**Name**, **Role / title**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Profile status** **Ready** or **Needs setup**, **Source**) and **Client Relationships** (client link; badges **Confirmed role**, **Needs role review**, **Profile-derived**).
3. Right panel (resizable; handle labelled "Resize side panel"): **Service Context** and **Communication History**.

**Done when:** H1 is the person's name. "Person not found" as H1 means a wrong id. **Contacts** (button, top) goes back.

### Fix the client role or details of a person
**Risk:** `READ` (handoff) · **Roles:** all

1. On the person profile, in **Client Relationships**, click **People setup** (button on the client row).
   - It opens `/clients/{slug}?tab=people&from=/people/{id}`.
2. Continue with [People and Roles tab](/clients/client-tabs-people-hr#people-and-roles-tab) (edit, confirm role, archive). Changes there are requests that need approval.

**Done when:** the client's **People & Roles** tab is open.

### Find clients missing a primary or HR payroll contact
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** all

1. On `/people`, click **Primary contact** or **HR/Payroll contact** in **Role coverage queue**, or the **Role gaps** tile.
   - It opens `/clients?view=profile-workspace&profile_gap=primary_contact` (or `hr_payroll_contact`).
2. Continue with [Fill missing profile data](/clients/clients-list#fill-missing-profile-data-profile-workspace).

The empty-state button **Add client people** opens `/clients?contact_filter=needs-coverage` ([Find clients with contact gaps](/clients/clients-list#find-clients-with-contact-gaps)).

### Save Gmail messages to the history of a person
**Risk:** `EXTERNAL` (reads your Gmail) then `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Admin. Operator: scanning works, saving returns 403.
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** person; which client relationship; which messages to keep.

1. Open `/people/{id}`.
2. In **Communication History**, choose the client (select, placeholder "Select client"; defaults to the first relationship).

<Warning>
**Approval required (EXTERNAL).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Scan Gmail**. It searches the signed-in user's Gmail for this person's email (last 180 days, up to 12 messages).
</Warning>

3. Click **Scan Gmail** (button; disabled when the person has no email; reads **Scanning...**).
   - Wait for: a list headed "Gmail matches ready for review" with a checkbox per message.
4. Tick the messages to keep.
5. Click `Save selected ({n})` (button; reads **Saving...**).

**Done when:** toast `{n} communication(s) saved.` and the messages appear under **Saved communications**.

<Warning>
**Operator:** `Save selected ({n})` is enabled but the API refuses it. Expect the red box "Your profile cannot change Clients and packets. Allowed: Principal, Admin." Do not retry.
</Warning>

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| "Add an email address before scanning Gmail for this person." | No email on the person | Add the email via the client's People & Roles tab (request) |
| "Unable to scan Gmail." or server text (red box) | Gmail not connected or the search failed | Stop; connecting Gmail is an OAuth step for a human |
| Nothing happens on **Scan Gmail** | The person has no client relationship | Report; a client link is needed first |

Empty states: "No saved communications yet.", "Loading communication history..."

## HubSpot contacts pages (not working)
`/contacts` and `/contacts/{id}` are a HubSpot contact browser with no navigation entry. Their API routes (`/api/contacts…`) are not mounted on the server, so expect:
- `/contacts`: H1 **Contacts**, description "Browse synced HubSpot contacts across client accounts.", tabs `All Contacts {n}`, `Unlinked {n}`, **Needs Review** (`?tab=all`, `unlinked`, `review`; the last tab used is remembered). Lists show "No contacts yet." because errors are swallowed.
- `/contacts/{id}`: "Loading contact..." then H1 **Contact not found** (or "Not Found").
- **New contact**, **Edit**, **Archive**, **Restore** open a change-request dialog whose submit fails with a red alert.
- **Export CSV** on the Unlinked tab fails.

Do not retry these. Use `/people` to read contacts and the client **People & Roles** tab to request changes. Report to Clarwiz that the contacts pages are unavailable.

### What the pages contain (for when the API is mounted)
**Risk:** `READ` (browse), `WRITE` (change request), `DESTRUCTIVE` (archive request) · **Roles:** no role gating in the UI

- **All Contacts** tab: card `Contacts ({n})`, search (placeholder "Search contacts...", waits 250 ms after typing), filters **Client** ("All clients") and **Lifecycle** ("All stages": **Subscriber**, **Lead**, **MQL**, **SQL**, **Opportunity**, **Customer**), **Clear all**, table **Name** (link to `/contacts/{id}`), **Role**, **Email**, **Client**, **Stage**, **Updated**, **Load more**.
- **Unlinked** tab: `Unlinked HubSpot contacts ({n})`, **Export CSV** (reads **Exporting...**; downloads a `hubspot-unlinked-contacts` CSV), search "Search unlinked contacts...", **Lifecycle**, **Load more**.
- **Needs Review** tab: text plus links **HubSpot sync health** (`/admin/integrations`) and **Open unlinked queue**.
- `/contacts/{id}` header: **Contacts** (back; defaults to `/people`), `{n} pending` badge (jumps to pending changes), **Edit**, **Archive** or **Restore**, **HubSpot** (opens HubSpot in a new tab). Cards **Person profile** (**Name**, **Lifecycle**, **Role**, **Client**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Notes**), **Service context** (**Client**, **Role**, **Reachability**), **Sync metadata** (**HubSpot contact ID**, **Updated**, **Created**), **History** ("Loading history…", "No history yet…").

Change-request dialog (titles **New contact (proposes change request)**, **Edit contact (proposes change request)**, **Archive contact**, **Restore contact**):
1. Fill **Name** (input, id "ccr-name", placeholder "Full name"; required to create; marked "(sensitive — owner approval)" on HubSpot contacts), **Email** (id "ccr-email", placeholder "name@example.com"), **Phone** (id "ccr-phone"), **Role / title** (id "ccr-role", placeholder "HR Director"), **Notes** (id "ccr-notes", placeholder "Internal notes — not synced to HubSpot.").
2. Fill **Reason** (id "ccr-reason"; placeholder "Context for the reviewer", "Why archive? (e.g., left the company)" or "Why restore?").

<Warning>
**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE)** for **Archive contact**: stop and get approval before clicking **Submit change request**.
</Warning>

3. Click **Submit change request** (button; reads **Submitting…**; disabled for create without a name, or edit with no change).

Done when the dialog closes. Errors show in a red alert with server text and possibly `Policy reason: {CODE}`. The request is only pending; approval happens on `/approvals`. No HubSpot write happens.

## Waits and signals
- `/people`: no spinner text; wait for tiles and rows. A failed load looks like an empty directory.
- `/people/{id}`: "Loading person profile..." then H1.
- **Scan Gmail**: button reads **Scanning...** until results show (a few seconds).

## Gotchas
- The sub-nav says **Contacts** but opens `/people`; the H1 on `/people` is also **Contacts**. Do not confuse it with the `/contacts` route.
- `/people` search placeholder mentions clients but client names are not searched (in-app help states this correctly).
- Opening a row is a full page load, not an in-app navigation; wait for the new page.
- Gmail scanning needs the user's Google account connected. If a Google sign-in or consent screen appears, stop and hand off to a human.
- Nothing on these pages sends email or writes to HubSpot. Email links open the local mail client; **HubSpot** buttons only open hubspot.com.
