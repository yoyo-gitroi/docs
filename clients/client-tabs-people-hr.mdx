<Info>
**URL:** `/clients/{slug}?tab=people`, `employees`, `payroll-tax`, `compliance`, `research` · **Reach:** client page → tab bar ([tab map](/clients/client-profile#tab-map)) · **Roles:** Principal ✓ Operator ✓ (People, Payroll and Research view only; Employees buttons disabled but row click edits; Compliance editable but **Start research** returns 403) · **Ready when:** the tab's H2 (**People & Roles**, **Employees**, **Payroll & Tax**, **Research**) or the **Compliance register** heading is visible and its loading text is gone.
</Info>

Five client tabs that hold people, staff and HR data, plus the shared **Import from document** dialog.

<Note>
Always open these with `?tab=`. Without it the client page jumps to the tab you last used ([Always pass ?tab=](/clients/client-profile#always-pass-the-tab-parameter)).
</Note>

## People and Roles tab
People at the client that Ariya works with. Nothing here edits a contact directly: every add, edit, archive, unlink and role change files a **contact change request** that a person approves on `/approvals` (**Approve**, then **Apply Local Change**). No HubSpot write happens from this tab. Edit needs Clients and packets: Operator and Member see "View only…" and disabled controls.

**Ready when:** H2 **People & Roles**, heading **Roles and contacts**, and the strip **Relationship changes** / **Approval required** with badges **No direct record mutation**, **Audit trail preserved**. Loading text "Loading people...".

Layout top to bottom: person insight cards (stats **median reply**, **agrees first time**, **last contact**); **Seen on case threads, not in HubSpot**; **Ariya team on this account**; **How people insights are built**; **Roles and contacts** header with **Import from document** and **People Directory**; **Change requests** (pending only); the request form card (title **Assign person to client**, **Request person/role edit**, **Request archive** or **Request unlink**); **Coverage resolver**; **Confirmed roles** (groups **Primary contact**, **Accounting**, **HR / Payroll / Benefits**, **Employees**, **Executives / Owners**, **Other people**); **Archived**.

Contact row icons (right end of each row in **Confirmed roles**, left to right; none has a label): **Use suggestion** (text button, only with a suggestion), pencil (edit), star (make primary; not on the current primary), broken chain (unlink), box (archive). Rows tagged **profile** have only the pencil.

### Request a new contact
**Risk:** `WRITE` (files a request) · **Roles:** Principal, Admin
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** name and/or email, client role; optional title, phone, notes, reason.

1. Open `/clients/{slug}?tab=people`.
   - Wait for: "Loading people..." to finish.
2. Scroll to the form card **Assign person to client**. If it shows another title, click **New Person** (button) to reset it.
3. Fill **Name** and/or **Email** (inputs). Optional **Title**, **Phone**, **Notes**.
4. Click **Client role** (select) and pick **Primary contact**, **Accounting**, **HR**, **Payroll**, **Benefits**, **Employee**, **Executive**, **Owner**, **Legal**, **Advisor** or **Other**.
5. Optional: **Reason / context** (textarea, placeholder "Optional").
6. Click **Request change** (button; reads **Submitting...**).

**Done when:** toast "Contact change request submitted." and a row under **Change requests** with badges for the action and **Pending**. The contact itself appears only after approval and apply on `/approvals`.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| "Contact requires a name or email" | Both empty | Fill one |
| "Action denied by policy" | 403 from the contact policy | Stop; report |
| "Client id is required before requesting contact changes." | Client not loaded | Reload the tab |
| `Contact coverage could not be loaded: {error}` on the resolver card | Same text as the submit error, not a load failure | Read it as the submit error |

### Request an edit to a contact
**Risk:** `WRITE` (files a request) · **Roles:** Principal, Admin

1. In **Confirmed roles**, click the pencil on the contact (icon button, no label; visual: first icon at the right end of the row).
   - Wait for: form title **Request person/role edit** with the contact's values.
2. Change the fields.
3. Click **Request change**.

**Done when:** toast "Contact change request submitted." Error "No contact changes to request" means nothing changed. "Fallback contacts must be added before requesting this change" means the row is a **profile** fallback; request a new contact instead.

### Make a contact the primary contact
**Risk:** `WRITE` (files a request) · **Roles:** Principal, Admin

1. Click the star on the contact (icon button, no label; visual: second icon at the right end of the row; absent on the current primary).
   - It files the request immediately; there is no form.

**Done when:** toast "Primary contact request submitted."

### Request to archive or unlink a contact
**Risk:** `DESTRUCTIVE` (files a request) · **Roles:** Principal, Admin

1. Click the box icon (archive) or the broken-chain icon (unlink) on the contact (icon buttons, no label; visual: last two icons at the right end of the row).
   - Wait for: form title **Request archive** or **Request unlink**; other fields are disabled.
2. Type the reason in **Reason** (textarea, placeholder "Required").

<Warning>
**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Request change**. This proposes removing the person from the client.
</Warning>

3. Click **Request change**.

**Done when:** toast "Contact change request submitted." Errors: "Archive requests require a reason", "Unlink requests require a reason".

### Confirm contact roles in the coverage resolver
**Risk:** `WRITE` (files requests) · **Roles:** Principal, Admin

1. Scroll to **Coverage resolver**.
   - Wait for: "Loading contact coverage..." to finish.
2. One contact: set the role (select, aria-label `Coverage role for {name}`), then click **Confirm role** (button on that row). Toast "Role confirmation submitted for approval."
3. Several: tick **Select** (checkbox per row) or tick **Select suggested roles** (checkbox, selects all rows with a suggestion), then click **Submit selected**. Toast `{n} role confirmation request(s) submitted for approval.`
4. **Use suggestion** on a row loads the suggestion into the form instead.

**Done when:** the toast shows. "A coverage role is required" means no role was chosen. Empty: "No active contacts are available for coverage review.", "No imported contacts need role confirmation."

HubSpot-imported contacts appear in **Confirmed roles** only after a role request is approved and applied.

### Request a case participant as a contact
**Risk:** `WRITE` (files a request; no HubSpot write despite the label) · **Roles:** Principal, Admin

1. In **Seen on case threads, not in HubSpot**, click **Add to HubSpot** (button on the person's row).

**Done when:** toast "Contact request submitted for approval." and the button reads **Requested**. The request uses the email prefix as the name and role "other". Empty: "Everyone writing on this client's cases is already a contact."

Other controls: **People Directory** (opens `/people`, see [Contacts directory](/clients/people-and-contacts)); **Import from document** (see [Import from a document](#import-from-a-document)).

## Employees tab
Everyone who works at the client. Contacts we talk to are on People & Roles.

<Warning>
**Operator:** **Add Employee**, **Import CSV**, **Import from document** and the row **Edit** buttons are disabled for you, but clicking a table row (not the button) still opens the edit sheet and the API accepts your save. **Member:** the row click opens the sheet too, but saving returns 403 in the sheet's red box; do not retry.
</Warning>

**Ready when:** H2 **Employees** and text `{n} employee(s)`. Loading text "Loading employees...".

Layout: tiles **Active**, **Hourly**, **Tipped**, **Salaried**; **By location**; buttons **Import CSV**, **Import from document**, **Add Employee**; table **Name**, **Email**, **Title**, **Department**, **Location**, **Pay basis**, **Tipped**, **Status**, **Start Date**, **Edit**. Empty: "No employees yet" / "Add individually or import a CSV."

### Add an employee
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Admin
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** first and last name, plus any other fields.

1. Open `/clients/{slug}?tab=employees`.
2. Click **Add Employee** (button; visual: above the table, right).
   - Wait for: side sheet **Add Employee**.
3. **Personal Information:** **First Name** and **Last Name** (required), **Middle Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Date of Birth**, **Start Date**, **Status** (select: **Active**, **Terminated**, **On Leave**).
4. **Job Information:** **Job Title**, **Department**, **Work location** (placeholder "e.g. Midtown"), **Receives tips** (checkbox), **Employment Type** (**W-2**, **1099 Contractor**), **Classification** (**Exempt**, **Non-Exempt**), **Job Type** (**Salaried**, **Hourly**, **Tipped**, **Commission**).
5. **Compensation & Tax:** **Rate of Pay** (placeholder `e.g. $25/hr or $52,000/yr`), **Pay Frequency**, **Filing Status** (**Single**, **Married Filing Jointly**, **Married Filing Separately**, **Head of Household**), **Dependents Credit**.
6. Click **Add Employee** (button, sheet footer; reads **Saving...**).

**Done when:** toast "Employee added" and the row appears. Error "First and last name are required" in the sheet.

### Edit an employee
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Admin; Operator via row click (see warning above)

1. Click **Edit** on the row (button, last column), or click the row itself.
   - Wait for: side sheet **Edit Employee**.
2. Change fields.
3. Click **Update Employee** (button, sheet footer).

**Done when:** toast "Employee updated".

There is no delete. To end employment, set **Status** to **Terminated**.

<Warning>
**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE).** Setting **Status** to **Terminated** deactivates the employee record. Stop and get approval before clicking **Update Employee** with that change.
</Warning>

### Import employees from a CSV
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Admin
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** a CSV file in the template's format.

1. Click **Import CSV** (button above the table).
   - Wait for: dialog **Import employees from CSV**.
2. Optional: **Download template** (button) to get the column layout.
3. Click **Choose CSV file** (button; hidden file input aria-label "CSV file") and pick the file.
   - Wait for: the report. `All {n} rows are valid and ready to import.` or `{n} rows have problems. Fix the file and choose it again — nothing has been imported.` Pills `{n} rows`, `{n} valid`, `{n} invalid`; lines `Missing required: …`, `Not allowed (sensitive): …`; an issue table **Row**, **Column**, **Problem**.
4. If there are problems, stop and report the issue table. **Choose another file** retries with a fixed file.
5. Click `Import {n} employee(s)` (button).

**Done when:** toast `{n} employee(s) imported`. "The server rejected the import:" followed by text means nothing was imported; report it. CSV import always adds new rows; it never updates existing employees.

## Payroll and Tax tab
What Ariya holds about the client's payroll setup. Edit needs Clients and packets: Operator and Member see "View only…".

**Ready when:** H2 **Payroll & Tax** and card **What we hold**. Loading text "Loading...".

### Update payroll and tax details
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Admin
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** the fields to change.

1. Open `/clients/{slug}?tab=payroll-tax`.
   - Wait for: "Loading..." to finish and the fields to show stored values. If the fields are all blank on a client that should have data, the load failed; stop and do not save.
2. **Tax Identification:** **Federal EIN** (placeholder "XX-XXXXXXX"), **State Withholding ID**, **State Unemployment (SUI) ID**. Tick **Operates in multiple states** to show rows **State (e.g. CA)** and **Tax ID**; **Add State** adds a row.
3. **Payroll Configuration:** **Pay Frequency**, **Payday** (placeholder "e.g. Friday, 15th & last"), **Time Tracking** (**Manual Entry**, **Time Clock**, **Timesheets**, **Other**), **W-2 Employees**, **1099 Contractors**, **Tip Reporting** (**Employee Declared**, **Employer Allocated**, **N/A**), **Employee Types** checkboxes (**Salaried**, **Hourly**, **Tipped**, **Commission**, **Piece Rate**, **Remote/Out-of-State**), **Deductions & Contributions** checkboxes (**Health Insurance**, **Retirement (401k/IRA)**, **Garnishments**, **Union Dues**).
4. **Banking Information:** **Bank Name**, **Account Number** (password field), **Routing Number**.
5. **System Access Points:** **Add User** adds a row with **Name**, **Email**, **Access Level** (**Full Admin**, **Payroll Only**, **Reports Only**, **Employee Self-Service**, **HR/Onboarding**, **Time & Attendance**).
6. Click **Save All Details** (button; reads **Saving...**, then **Saved**).

**Done when:** toast and alert "Details saved." Failure: red alert with server text.

<Warning>
**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE)** if your edit removed a state row or a system access user (trash icons, no label). Rows vanish only on screen until **Save All Details**, which replaces everything stored with what is on screen. Get approval before saving such a removal.
</Warning>

## Import from a document
One dialog on three tabs: **People & Roles** (`Import people & roles from a document`), **Employees** (`Import employees from a document`), **Payroll & Tax** (`Import payroll & tax details`). AI reads one file and proposes values; nothing is saved until you apply.

### Import values from a document
**Risk:** `AI` (extract) then `WRITE` (apply) · **Roles:** Principal, Admin (button disabled for Operator and Member)
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** one file (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, text or image, up to 10 MB).

1. Open the tab (`?tab=people`, `?tab=employees` or `?tab=payroll-tax`) and click **Import from document** (button; visual: tab header or above the table).
   - Wait for: dialog with "Upload a document and AI will pull out the values that belong on this tab. Nothing is saved until you review and apply."
2. Click **Drop a file or click to upload** (button) and pick the file. Remove it with the × (icon button, aria-label "Remove file").
3. Click **Extract values** (button). It reads **Reading document…** until done; this is one request and can take up to a minute.
   - Wait for: description `We read {file} with AI. Here is what we could extract for {client}. Check each value before applying.`
4. Review:
   - Payroll: each field row has a checkbox `Apply {label}`, a badge **New**, **Changes value** or **Already on file**, an editor `{label} (extracted)`, `On file now: …`, and an evidence quote.
   - People and Employees: each record has a checkbox `Apply {title}`, a badge **New contact**, **New employee** or `Updates existing (by …)`, a field count, and for unclear matches a select `Match for {title}` with `Add as a new {noun}`.
   - Untick anything wrong. Edit values where needed.
5. Read the apply note: People "Nothing changes directly: each person becomes a change request in the approval queue."; Employees "New employees are added. For matched employees only the selected fields are updated."; Payroll "Applying saves the selected values to Tax Identification, Payroll Configuration and System Access. Unsaved edits on this tab are replaced."
6. Click `Apply {n} {unit}` (button; disabled at 0; reads **Applying…**).

**Done when:** toast `{n} {unit} applied from {file}.` (people: `sent for approval`). Partial: `…; {n} could not be applied.`

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| "File must be 10MB or smaller." | File too large | Use a smaller file |
| `No values for this tab were found in {file}.` | Nothing relevant | Report; try another file |
| "This upload was already applied or discarded." | Dialog was closed or reused after extraction | Start again with a new upload |
| Toast with server text | Extraction or apply failed (or rate limit) | Report the text |

Bank account numbers, routing numbers and Social Security numbers are never extracted, by policy. **Upload a different file** discards the current extraction. Closing the dialog also discards it.

## Compliance tab
The client's compliance register: which obligations apply, their status and what to do.

<Warning>
**Operator:** this tab is editable for you and **Run research** / **Start research** are enabled, but the API refuses the run (Principal and Admin only). Expect a toast "Your profile cannot change Clients and packets. Allowed: Principal, Admin." Do not retry; report back. Member: view only.
</Warning>

**Ready when:** heading **Compliance register** (inside a card; this tab has no H2 banner). Before the first run: `No register yet for {client}. A run works out which obligations apply…` with **Run research**. Loading: pulsing block (aria-busy). Load error: "Could not load the compliance register." with **Try again**.

### Run compliance research
**Risk:** `AI` · **Roles:** Principal, Admin (Operator: 403)
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** optional as-of date, expiry horizon, timeline, mode.

1. Open `/clients/{slug}?tab=compliance`.
2. Click **Run research** (button; visual: register header card, or the empty-state card).
   - Wait for: dialog with `Research covers {client} only…`.
3. Optional: **As of** (date, default today), **Expiry horizon** (**30**, **60**, **90**, **180**; default 90), **Timeline** (**6**, **12**, **18**; default 12), **Mode** (**Auto**, **Client**, **Prospect**; hint explains Auto's choice), **Model**.
4. Click **Start research** (button; reads **Starting**).
   - Wait for: toast "Research started. The register updates here when it finishes." The header button reads **Research running** (disabled), chip `Research running since {time}`, and **Behind the scenes** shows a **Live** badge.
5. Wait. A run takes several minutes (15 to 40 web searches). The page checks every 5 seconds; do not reload or start another run.

**Done when:** toast `Register updated. {n} new, {n} changed, {n} resolved.` or "Register ready." and the page scrolls to **The register**.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| "A compliance run is already in progress for this client." | Run already going | Wait for it |
| "The research run failed." or run error toast, and red callout `The research run from {date}, {time} failed.` | Run failed | Click **Run again** once; report if it fails again |
| "Could not start the research run." or server text | Start rejected (Operator: 403) | Stop; report |
| Warning "No local ordinance table is loaded…" in the dialog | Local wage data missing | Report it along with results |

### Read the compliance register
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** all

1. Open `/clients/{slug}?tab=compliance`.
2. Header chips: **Mode** (Client or Prospect), `{n} employees`, `HR Intel {n} primary, {n} commentary`, optional **Ordinance table stale**, `Checks {x} of {y} passed` (click for dialog **Register checks**).
3. **Status counts** tiles (toggle buttons): **Expired**, **Outdated by law change**, **Expiring soon**, **New requirement**, **Coming into force**, **Not evidenced on file**, **Ask on call**, **Needs confirmation**, **In place**, **Not applicable**.
4. **The register**: tabs (tablist aria-label "Buckets") **Expiring or outdated**, **New and required**, **Needs confirmation**, **In place**, **Not applicable**; click **Show all in this bucket** for the full list. Clicking an item opens its details (**Identity**, **Trigger**, **Evidence**, **Lifecycle**, **What changed**, **Action**, **If missed**, **Citations**).
5. Also on the page: **Changes since the last run**, **Wage floor by location**, `Next {n} months`, **Discovery call guide** (prospects), **Document acceptance check**, **Recommended services**, **Documents to create or update**, **Open questions**.
6. `Sources {n}` (button) opens a sources side sheet. **JSON** (button) opens dialog **complianceRegister JSON** with **Copy JSON** (toast "JSON copied", or "Copy was blocked. Select the JSON and copy it by hand.").

**Done when:** you have what Clarwiz asked for.

## Research tab
Public information about the client, each finding tied to a source. Facts are reviewed, then saved to the company file. Edit needs Clients and packets: Operator and Member see "View only…".

**Ready when:** H2 **Research** and card **AI Research** with **Add Research**. Loading text "Loading research history…".

### Run client research
**Risk:** `AI` · **Roles:** Principal, Admin

1. Open `/clients/{slug}?tab=research`.
2. Click **Add Research** (button; visual: **AI Research** card header; reads **Starting…**).
   - Wait for: toast "Research run started" and the **Latest run** badge **Research queued** then **Researching client**, plus a card **Researching client** that says the page refreshes automatically (every 3 seconds).
3. Wait. Do not click **Add Research** again; it stays enabled and would start a second run.

**Done when:** **Latest run** badge reads **Ready for review**. **Needs attention** means it failed: the card **Research needs attention** has **Retry** (starts a fresh run). "Failed to start research" means the start was rejected.

### Review and save research facts
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Admin
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** which facts to keep (or "all that look right").

1. Open `/clients/{slug}?tab=research` and scroll to `Review queue ({n})`.
2. Tick facts (checkbox, aria-label `Select suggestion {fieldPath}`).
3. Click `Mark reviewed ({n})` (button, aria-label "Mark selected facts reviewed"). Toast `Marked reviewed {n}`.
4. Click `Save to company file ({n})` (aria-label "Save selected reviewed facts to company file") or `Review + save ({n})` (aria-label "Review and save selected facts to company file") to do both steps at once.
   - Wait for: dialog **Save reviewed facts to company file?** (or **Review and save facts to company file?**) listing **Current** and **Will save** per item.
5. Click `Save {n} fact(s)` or `Review + save {n} fact(s)` (button in the dialog).

**Done when:** toast `Saved to company file {n}` or `Reviewed and saved {n}`, and the facts move to `Now in Company File ({n})`. Partial: `{ok} ok, {n} failed: {code}`. Each fact's `Goes to: {destination}` link opens the tab it updates (Company Profile, Notes, Memory, Jurisdictions).

Per-row buttons do the same one fact at a time: **Mark reviewed**, **Review + save**, **Save to company file**. A badge **Needs edit before apply** disables them until the fact is edited.

### Edit a research suggestion
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Admin

1. Click **Edit** on the fact row.
2. Change **Proposed value JSON** (must be valid JSON), **Confidence** (0 to 1), **Rationale**, **Edit reason**.
3. Click **Save**. **Cancel** discards.

**Done when:** the row shows the new value. Errors: "Proposed value must be valid JSON", "Confidence must be a number from 0 to 1".

### Reject research facts
**Risk:** `DESTRUCTIVE` (no undo in the app) · **Roles:** Principal, Admin

1. Tick the facts in **Review queue**.

<Warning>
**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Reject**. Rejected facts cannot be brought back from the app.
</Warning>

2. Click `Reject ({n})` (button, aria-label "Reject selected") or **Reject** on a single row.

**Done when:** toast `Rejected {n}` and the facts move to `Rejected Research Facts ({n})`.

Other research controls: run date buttons in **Run history** (switch run; runs from the compliance register carry a **Compliance register** badge); `[{id}]` source anchors (aria-label `Jump to research source {id}`); **Exact values** (expands JSON).

## Waits and signals
- Compliance run: several minutes; page polls every 5 seconds; **Behind the scenes** has a **Live** badge while running; finished on the "Register updated…" or "Register ready." toast.
- Research run: polls every 3 seconds; finished when **Latest run** reads **Ready for review** (or **Needs attention**).
- Import from document: **Reading document…** on the button until the review step appears.
- Change requests return immediately with a toast; the contact changes only after approval on `/approvals`.

## Gotchas
- People & Roles never edits contacts directly; tell Clarwiz the change is pending approval.
- The People tab's pending list shows only requests within the latest 100 pending tenant-wide.
- Contact row icons have no labels; use their position.
- Employees: a row click opens the edit sheet even when the tab is view-only.
- Payroll & Tax: never save after a failed load; blank fields would overwrite stored values.
- Compliance and Research buttons look available to Operators, but the runs are refused by the server.
- **Add Research** stays enabled during a run; a second click starts a second run.
