## Recipes

### Check Data Health
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** none.
**Preconditions:** Principal account.

1. Open `/data-health` (no menu entry; also reachable from `/getting-started` step 1 and **Open Data Health** on `/crm-hygiene`).
   - Wait for: `Loading Data Health...` to be replaced by `Generated {date}`.
2. Read the summary line, the banners, `Scheduled sync daemon` and the `HubSpot CRM` `Blockers` list.
3. To re-read, click **Refresh** (button; visual: page header, right).

**Done when:** `Generated {date}` and the cards are visible.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| `Failed to load Data Health: {error}` | Load failed or your profile is not Principal | Use the Principal account; otherwise report. |

### Run a HubSpot sync
**Risk:** `EXTERNAL` `WRITE` (reads HubSpot, changes Ariya client and contact rows) · **Roles:** Principal
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** approval.
**Preconditions:** `/data-health` loaded.

1. Open `/data-health` and wait for `Generated {date}`.
2. Read `External writes` in the `HubSpot CRM` card and report it with the approval request.

<Warning>**Approval required (EXTERNAL).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Sync HubSpot**. It starts at once with no confirm dialog and can change many client and contact rows.</Warning>

3. Click **Sync HubSpot** (button; visual: page header, left of **Refresh**).
   - Wait for: the icon to stop spinning. It can take several minutes.

**Done when:** toast `HubSpot sync completed: {n} company rows and {m} contact rows changed.` (or warning `HubSpot sync finished with issues: {n} blocker(s).`) and a result card with `Status`, `Companies changed`, `Contacts changed`, `HubSpot writes` (`Enabled` or `Off`).

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Toast and card `HubSpot sync failed: {error}` | Sync failed | Report the text. Do not retry without approval. |
| `HubSpot sync finished with issues: {n} blocker(s).` | Partial | Report the blockers listed in the card. |

### Review unlinked CRM contacts
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** optional tab, domain, lifecycle stage, search text.
**Preconditions:** Principal account.

1. Open `/crm-hygiene?tab={tab}` where tab is `all`, `needs-review` (**With suggestions**), `no-suggestion` or `skipped`.
   - Wait for: tab labels with counts and the queue table or its empty text.
2. Optional scope: click **Domain** (input, placeholder "e.g. acme.com") and type a domain (clear with the X button, aria-label "Clear domain filter"); pick **Imported** (native select: `All time`, `Last 24h`, `Last 7 days`, `Last 30 days`). **Clear scope** resets both.
3. Optional filter: type in the search box (placeholder "Search unlinked contacts..."; waits 250 ms after typing) and pick **Lifecycle** (`All stages`, `Subscriber`, `Lead`, `MQL`, `SQL`, `Opportunity`, `Customer`).
4. Read rows. **Suggested client** shows a badge linking to `/clients/{slug}` or `No exact domain match`.
5. Click **Load more** until it disappears if you need every row.

**Done when:** the table shows the rows you need, or `No unlinked contacts match the current filters.`

<Note>Unverified at runtime: the contacts API this queue calls does not appear to be mounted on the server, so the queue and the domain card may show `Failed to load CRM hygiene data: Not Found` or `Domain breakdown unavailable: Not Found`. Report it rather than retrying.</Note>

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| `Failed to load CRM hygiene data: {error}` | Queue failed | Report. |
| `Domain breakdown unavailable: {error}` | Domain card failed | Report. |
| `Policy state unavailable: {error}` | Data Health part failed (often non-Principal) | Use the Principal account. |

This page is read-only. Linking contacts to clients happens in HubSpot, not here.

### Drill into one email domain
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** the domain.
**Preconditions:** none.

1. Open `/crm-hygiene?tab=needs-review&emailDomain={domain}` directly. Or on the **All unlinked** tab, in `Top unlinked email domains`, click the arrow link at the end of the domain row (badges `matches client`, `personal`, `no client match`).

**Done when:** the **With suggestions** tab is active with the **Domain** scope set.

### Export unlinked contacts as CSV
**Risk:** `READ` (downloads a file) · **Roles:** Principal
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** tab and filters.
**Preconditions:** queue loaded.

1. Set the tab and filters as in [Review unlinked CRM contacts](#review-unlinked-crm-contacts).
2. Click **Export CSV** (button; visual: queue card header).
   - Wait for: `Exporting...` to return to **Export CSV**.

**Done when:** a file named `hubspot-unlinked-contacts{suffix}.csv` downloads.

### See contacts skipped by lifecycle policy
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** none.
**Preconditions:** none.

1. Open `/crm-hygiene?tab=skipped`.
   - Wait for: card `Skipped by lifecycle policy` (fallbacks `Loading lifecycle policy...`, `Lifecycle policy unavailable.`).
2. Use **Open Data Health for sync details** or **HubSpot integration health** (opens `/admin/integrations`) for more.

**Done when:** the policy card is visible.

### Compare two versions of a document
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** document ID (or title) and the two version numbers.
**Preconditions:** the document has at least two versions.

1. Open `/compare?doc={documentId}&from={v}&to={v}`. Without `from` and `to` the page compares oldest to latest. Alternative: on the document's History sheet click **Compare any two versions →** (shown only with 2 or more versions).
   - Wait for: `Loading...` to clear and the summary `v{from} · {time} → v{to} · {time}` with added, removed, changed and unchanged counts.
2. To change the document, click **Document** (dropdown, placeholder `Select a document...`; options sorted by title) and pick one.
3. To change versions, click **From version** or **To version** and pick `v{n} — {edit description}`. The URL updates as you change selects.
4. Tick **Show unchanged rows** to include unchanged fields.
5. Read the table: `Status` is `added`, `removed`, `changed` or `unchanged`; empty values show `(empty)`; values longer than 240 characters are cut (hover for the full text).
6. **Open document** opens `/documents/{id}`.

**Done when:** the table or `No changes between these two versions.` is visible.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| `Pick a document to compare versions.` | No `doc` in URL | Pick a document. |
| `This document has only {n} version(s) — nothing to compare yet.` | Single version | Report. |
| `Failed to load documents` / `Failed to load versions` | Load failed | Reload once; report. |

### Find a trusted source
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal (internal tenant)
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** filters (status, freshness, trust, scope, country, topic).
**Preconditions:** Principal in tenant `ariya` or `pilot`.

1. Open `/admin/trusted-sources` (or Intelligence tab **Trusted Sources**, or **Manage trusted sources** on `/hr-intel`).
   - Wait for: `Loading trusted sources...` to be replaced by `{visible} source shown from {total} catalog records.`
2. Set filters in the catalog card. Each change reloads at once:
   - **Status**: `All`, `Active`, `Needs review`, `Marked stale`, `Archived`.
   - **Freshness**: `All`, `Fresh`, `Due soon`, `Stale`, `Needs review`, `Archived`.
   - **Trust**: `All`, `Primary authority`, `Secondary authority`, `Legal analysis`, `Professional`, `Vendor`, `News`, `Internal`, `Web`.
   - **Scope**: `All`, `Federal`, `State`, `Local`, `Professional`, `Vendor`, `Internal`.
   - **Country** (placeholder "US") and **Topic** (placeholder "wage-hour") reload on every keystroke; type the whole value, then wait 2 seconds.
3. **Open source** ↗ on a row opens the source in a new tab.

**Done when:** the table shows the matching rows, or `No trusted sources match the current filters.`

### Edit a trusted source
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal (internal tenant)
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** the source and the field values.
**Preconditions:** source found.

1. Click **Edit** on the row.
   - Wait for: the `Edit source` card at the **bottom of the page**. Scroll down.
2. Change fields: **Name**, **Publisher**, **URL**, **Trust**, **Scope**, **Status** (`Active`, `Needs review`, `Stale`, `Archived`), **Country**, **Region**, **Locality**, **Owner**, **Refresh cadence days**, **Topics** (comma-separated).
3. Click **Save source** (disabled until Name, Publisher, Trust, Scope, Country, Owner, a cadence above 0 and at least one topic are set). **Cancel** discards.
   - Wait for: `Saving...` to finish.

**Done when:** toast `Trusted source updated`. The card's `Review history` lists events as `{eventType} by {actor} on {date}` or `No review events recorded yet.`

### Mark a trusted source reviewed
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal (internal tenant)
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** the source; optional review note.
**Preconditions:** source found.

1. Click **Review** on the row.
   - Wait for: a **native browser prompt** `Review note for {name}` with default text `Reviewed source page and metadata.`
2. Keep or replace the note, then accept the prompt (OK). Dismissing it cancels.

**Done when:** toast `Source marked reviewed`.

### Archive a trusted source
**Risk:** `DESTRUCTIVE` (soft) · **Roles:** Principal (internal tenant)
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** the source; approval.
**Preconditions:** the source is not already archived.

<Warning>**Approval required (DESTRUCTIVE).** Stop here and get approval before clicking **Archive**. An archived source stops grounding HR Intel answers.</Warning>

1. Click **Archive** on the row.
   - Wait for: a **native browser confirm** `Archive {name}?`
2. Accept (OK).

**Done when:** toast `Source archived` and the row disappears from non-archived filters.

## Waits and signals
- Data Health: `Loading Data Health...`; a HubSpot sync spins the button icon and can run for minutes; the result arrives as a toast plus a result card.
- CRM Hygiene: tab counts appear when loaded; search waits 250 ms after typing; **Load more** shows `Loading...`.
- Compare: `Loading...` while versions load; selects are disabled until versions arrive.
- Trusted Sources: `Loading trusted sources...`; every filter change reloads the table.

## Gotchas
- `/data-health` is the Admin profile's default landing page, but its data is Principal-only.
- **Sync HubSpot** has no confirm dialog.
- The `HubSpot CRM` card shows sub-section titles (`Live read preview`, `Dry-run audit record`, `Manual HubSpot sync`, `HubSpot review queue`, `HubSpot import readiness`) but their action buttons are not rendered on this page.
- CRM Hygiene does no linking; it only shows and exports.
- Trusted Sources uses native browser prompt and confirm dialogs; your automation must accept or dismiss them.
- The `Edit source` card appears at the bottom of the page, not beside the row.
- **Country** and **Topic** filters fire a request per keystroke.
- These pages have no in-app guide of their own; **?** opens `How Ariya fits together`.
