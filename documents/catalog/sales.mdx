<Info>
**Reference page** · **Live source of truth:** sidebar **Documents** → **Templates** tab (`/documents/templates`), one template at `/documents/templates/{slug}` · **Roles:** Principal ✓ Operator ✓ can create documents from these templates; only a Principal (or Admin) can fork, edit, lock or deactivate a template · **Baseline:** what the repo seeds; your tenant may differ.
</Info>

The **Sales** category holds 7 seeded platform templates. Tenant `ariya` also has a **Billing Memo** in this category (shown as "Sales").

<Warning>
Four templates answer to the word "proposal": **HR Proposal** (`proposal`), **Services Proposal**, **Consulting Proposal** and **Workers' Comp Proposal**. A bare "proposal" request matches `proposal`; the assistant must confirm which one. Always say the full title.
</Warning>

<Note>
Every good request names a client because the Document Assistant will not draft until a client is bound, even for internal pieces such as flyers. Example people, amounts and dates are fictional; swap in the user's facts. Facts you leave out become flagged gaps or a follow-up question, never invented values.
</Note>

## How to read an entry

- **Fields, by form section** uses the labels the template form shows. "(list; each row: …)" is a repeatable list with an add button. Types other than text are shown in brackets, for example (date) or (number); "(choice: …)" lists the dropdown options.
- **Starts blank** fields have no default and no client value, and their label does not say "optional". The assistant reports each one as a gap, or asks when it is a true blocker such as the person's name.
- **Blank unless the client profile has it** fields fill themselves from the client record. Put the value in the request only if the client profile lacks it.
- All other fields start with a default from the template. The assistant only changes a default when it has a source for the new value.

## HR sales documents

### HR Proposal

**Slug:** `proposal` · **Kind:** Handlebars · **Scope:** tenant · **Person to name:** none

Multi-page HR services proposal: pain points, recommended tier, comparison, next steps.

- **From the client profile:** company name, signer / contact name, contact title, employee count, industry
- **Dated automatically at creation:** Date

**Fields, by form section:**

- **Header:** Title (use \*italics\* for emphasis); Subtitle; Prepared by; Date (date); Validity (days) (number)
- **Client:** Client name; Contact name; Contact title; Employee count; Industry
- **Narrative:** Cover tagline; Pain-points footer note; Compliance exposure \$; Risk callout body; ROI body
- **Pain points** (list; each row: Title; Detail)
- **Tier (Ariya offerings):** Apply a tier (choice: — pick a tier to auto-fill recommendation & fee —)
- **Recommendation:** Recommended tier (choice: HR OnDemand / Elevated HR); Recommended monthly fee; Go-live timeline; Compliance timeline
- **Tier table headings:** Basic tier label; Premium tier label; PEPM footnote
- **Tier comparison rows** (list; each row: Feature; Basic value; Premium value)
- **Add-ons** (list; each row: Add-on)
- **Next steps** (list; each row: Action; Timeline)
- **CTA:** CTA body
- **Fillable PDF acceptance fields:** Acceptance note; Authorized signer name; Authorized signer title; Authorized signature; Signature date (date)

**Starts blank (the assistant asks or flags these):** Prepared by; Compliance exposure \$; Apply a tier; Recommended monthly fee; Authorized signer name; Authorized signer title; Authorized signature; Signature date

**Blank unless the client profile has it:** Client name; Contact name; Contact title; Employee count; Industry

**Good request:** "Create an HR Proposal for ABC Dental: prepared by Rachel Kim; compliance exposure 25,000 USD; tier Elevated HR at 1,200 USD a month; authorized signer Dr. Sam Patel, Owner; leave signature and signature date blank for signing."

### Services Proposal

**Slug:** `services-proposal` · **Kind:** Handlebars · **Scope:** tenant · **Person to name:** none

Ariya HR services proposal — why Ariya, onboarding steps, payroll core bundle, optional modules, Elevated HR, itemized pricing.

- **From the client profile:** company name, signer / contact name, employee count, industry
- **Dated automatically at creation:** Date

**Fields, by form section:**

- **Prepared for:** Client company; Client short name; City, State; Employees; Attn; Date (date)
- **Positioning:** Industry; Industry experience; Accounting system
- **Payroll Core bundle** (list; each row: Bold lead-in; Detail)
- **Optional modules** (list; each row: Module; Description)
- **Elevated HR inclusions** (list; each row: Bold lead-in; Detail)
- **Investment:** Discount applied to every line (%) (number)
- **Itemized pricing** (list; each row: Line item; Sub-line; List price; Client price)
- **Example monthly totals** (list; each row: Configuration; Maths; Monthly total)
- **Notes:** Deal notes

**Starts blank (the assistant asks or flags these):** Client short name; City, State; Industry experience; Accounting system; Deal notes

**Blank unless the client profile has it:** Client company; Employees; Attn; Industry

**Good request:** "Create a Services Proposal for ABC Dental: short name 'ABC', Sacramento, CA, attn Dr. Sam Patel; industry experience: 12 dental practices served; accounting system QuickBooks Online; deal notes: 10% first-year discount."

### Discovery Summary

**Slug:** `discovery-summary` · **Kind:** Handlebars · **Scope:** tenant · **Person to name:** none

Short post-call recap: what we heard, where we would focus first, open questions.

- **From the client profile:** company name, signer / contact name, contact title, industry
- **Dated automatically at creation:** Call date, Prepared date
- **Team-member dropdown:** **Prepared by**; **Our attendees → Name** (pick a user from your team; the matching email fills itself; the `/generate` form shows a plain text box instead)

**Fields, by form section:**

- **Header:** Title; Call date (date); Prepared by; Prepared date (date)
- **Client:** Client name; Primary contact name; Primary contact title; Industry
- **Their attendees** (list; each row: Name; Role)
- **Our attendees** (list; each row: Name; Role)
- **What we heard** (list; each row: Item)
- **Featured quote (optional):** Quote; Attributed to
- **Where we would focus first** (list; each row: Area; Detail)
- **Open questions** (list; each row: Question)
- **Closing:** Next step; Closing

**Starts blank (the assistant asks or flags these):** Prepared by

**Blank unless the client profile has it:** Client name; Primary contact name; Primary contact title; Industry

**Good request:** "Create a Discovery Summary for ABC Dental: call 2026-10-29, prepared by Rachel Kim; their attendee Dr. Sam Patel (Owner), ours Rachel Kim (Consultant); what we heard and open questions from the attached call notes."

## Tenant ariya only

### Billing Memo

**Slug:** `billing-memo` · **Kind:** Legacy authored (fields plus prose sections) · **Scope:** client · **Person to name:** none · **Status:** seeded only for tenant `ariya`; generated by another module

Implementation billing memo used by the Implementation module. Other tenants do not have this row.

**Fields:** company data; contract setup; products and pricing (Product 1 to 3: Name, Qty, PEPM, Flat Rate, Misc.); Additional Pricing Notes.

**How it is filled:** the Implementation module builds it from implementation intake. The module uses any `billing-memo` or `billing-memo-*` row, or the template locked in **Settings** → **Implementation Catalog**. Legacy templates write prose sections with an LLM and turn unknown facts into fillable placeholders.

**Note:** Shares routing words with `implementation-billing-memo` ("iSolved Billing Memo", Business page). In tenant `ariya`, a title match on "billing memo" lands on this row first.

**Good request:** none. Do not request this template by hand; generate it from the Implementation module.
