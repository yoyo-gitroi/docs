**URL:** `/documents` then `/documents/chat/{sessionId}` · **Reach:** sidebar **Documents** · **Roles:** Principal ✓ Operator ✓ (Admin and Member too; no differences on this flow) · **Ready when:** the heading **What do you need?** and the box with placeholder "For example: an offer letter for Dana at Brightwater Dental, starting 6 October, $46.50 an hour" are visible.

This is the main recipe for producing a document. The AI document chat is the only path that writes real content from a plain-language request such as "create an incident report for Rachel at ABC Dental". Everything else on this page is a variant for special cases.

## Fast path

1. Open `/documents?client={clientSlug}` (or open `/documents` and pick the client in **Select a client…**).
2. Type the whole request, with every fact, into the request box. Click **Send**. Do not reload.
3. Wait until the URL is `/documents/chat/{sessionId}`, then check every 5 s until the turn is over: the message box is enabled and the hint next to **Add image** reads "Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line". This takes 30 s to several minutes (hard limit 10 min).
4. If a **Blockers** card appears, answer every question and click **Send answers**. If the last reply asks a question in prose, type the answer and click **Send**. Wait again.
5. When the **Live preview** shows the document, read it. Type any changes into the message box, **Send**, wait again.
6. Click **PDF (editable)** in the preview header. Wait for the toast "editable PDF downloaded".
7. Only if the chat came from an Operations case: click **Attach to case**. Wait for the URL to start with `/service-desk?case=`.
8. Report back: chat URL, document title, the **Open in editor** URL, the downloaded file name, and any gaps the assistant listed.

## Writing a good request

The assistant only stops to ask about true blockers (for example the employee's name on an offer letter). Everything else it does not know stays blank and is listed as a gap. It never invents names, dates, amounts, addresses or legal citations. So the quality of the document depends almost entirely on what you put in the first message.

- **Put every fact in one message.** Who, which client, dates and times, what happened, witnesses, action taken, pay, who completes or signs it. One complete message is faster than several follow-ups, because each message is a full turn of up to several minutes.
- **Start with a verb.** Begin with "Draft a …" or "Create a …". A request that reads like a question ("what goes in an offer letter?") gets a prose answer and no document.
- **Use the full document name** as it appears in Documents → Templates: "HR Incident Report", "Offer Letter", "Written Warning", "non-disclosure agreement". Abbreviations and slang ("NDA", "PIP", "write-up", "QBR", "DE 48") match templates poorly and trigger questions. See [Choosing a template](/documents/choosing-a-template).
- **Name the client exactly** as it is spelled in Ariya, or better, bind it with **Select a client…** before sending.
- **Give full names with job titles**, dates with day, month and year, times with am or pm, and money with the unit ("\$46.50 per hour", "\$85,000 per year").
- **Say who completes or signs it** ("Completed by Jane Smith, HR Manager").
- **Say what to leave blank** if you know it is missing ("Leave the response deadline blank"). This avoids a blocker question.
- **Attach source material** (an email, notes, a complaint) with **Attach files** instead of paraphrasing it. Non-image files need a bound client.
- **Expect a confirmation question** when several templates share a word: warnings (Written Warning vs Corrective Action Warning), incident reports, proposals, service agreements, handbooks. Answer with the exact template name Clarwiz gave you; if Clarwiz did not say, stop and ask.

Example requests (copy the shape, replace the facts):

Incident report:

```text
Draft an HR Incident Report for ABC Dental. Employee involved: Rachel Kim, dental assistant. Incident date and time: 28 October 2026, about 2:00 pm. Location: staff break room. What happened: Rachel reported a verbal altercation with a coworker, Tom Lee, about shift scheduling; voices were raised, there was no physical contact. Witness: Maria Diaz, front desk. Action taken at the scene: the two were separated and the office manager was notified. Injuries: none. Police or emergency services: not called. Completed by Jane Smith, HR Manager. Reported to Ariya HR on 28 October 2026.
```

Offer letter:

```text
Draft an Offer Letter for Brightwater Dental. Candidate: Dana Lopez, dana.lopez@example.com. Position title: Dental Hygienist. Position summary: provides hygiene care and patient education. Department: Clinical. Reports to: Dr. Amy Chen. Start date: 6 October 2026. Schedule: Monday to Thursday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm. Classification: non-exempt, hourly, $46.50 per hour. Response deadline: 25 September 2026. Signatory: Amy Chen, Practice Owner.
```

Written warning:

```text
Draft a Written Warning for ABC Dental, warning level Written Warning. Employer city and state: Sacramento, CA. Employee: Marcus Reed, Front Desk Coordinator, Front Office department, supervisor Jane Smith. Issue type: attendance and punctuality. Issue: arrived 20 to 40 minutes late for his 8:00 am shift on 6, 13 and 20 October 2026 without calling in. Prior warnings: verbal warning on 29 September 2026 for lateness. Required corrective action: arrive by 8:00 am for every scheduled shift and call the office manager before 7:30 am if running late. Improvement deadline: 30 days. Follow-up review: 20 November 2026.
```

Policy:

```text
Draft a Remote Work and Expense Reimbursement Policy for ABC Dental. Policy owner: Jane Smith, HR Manager. Effective date: 1 January 2027. Eligible roles: billing and scheduling staff, with manager approval; clinical roles are not eligible. Equipment: the company provides a laptop and headset. Expense reimbursement: internet and phone at $50 per month, claimed monthly with receipts. Timekeeping: non-exempt staff clock in and out in the payroll system and take all meal and rest breaks.
```

## Recipes

### Create a document from a request
**Risk:** `WRITE` `AI` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** client name or slug; document type; all facts; optional files; optional case id (see [From an Operations case](#variant-from-an-operations-case)).
**Preconditions:** signed in; the sidebar shows **Documents**.

1. Open `/documents` (or click sidebar **Documents**, then the top tab **Documents**).
   - Wait for: heading **What do you need?**, the request box, the **Select a client…** button, **Attach files** and **Send** (Send is disabled while the box is empty). The list below finishes loading when "Loading your documents…" disappears.
   - If instead you see "The document assistant is turned off for this workspace. You can still open a template below and generate from it." the chat is unavailable. Use [Generate now](#variant-from-a-template-with-generate-now).
2. Click **Select a client…** (combobox button, `aria-haspopup="listbox"`; visual: under the request box, left side). Skip this step if you opened `/documents?client={clientSlug}` and the button already shows the client name.
3. Type part of the client name or slug into the search box (aria-label "Search clients", placeholder "Search clients…"; visual: top of the dropdown). It matches anywhere in the name, so "King" finds "Delta King Hotel".
4. Click the client's name in the list (role `option`). If exactly one match shows, Enter also picks it. "No client matches that." means no match: stop and confirm the client with Clarwiz.
   - Done when: the picker button shows the client name. There is no clear option; to undo a wrong pick, reload `/documents` and start again.
5. Click the request box (textarea, label "Your request", id `document-ask`; visual: large box under the heading). Type the whole request (see [Writing a good request](#writing-a-good-request)). Use Shift+Enter for line breaks; plain Enter sends.
6. Optional: attach files. Click **Attach files** (button with paperclip; visual: under the request box). It opens the OS file picker on a hidden multi-file input; set the files on that input. Staged files show as chips with an "×" (aria-label `Remove {filename}`) and the button changes to **Add more**.
   - Images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF; up to 4; up to 20MB each) go with the first message. Other files (PDF, .docx, .eml, Excel, CSV, text; up to 10MB) are uploaded as client sources when the chat opens, and need a client from step 4.
7. Click **Send** (button, text "Send" with a paper-plane icon; visual: right end under the request box).
   - Wait for: the Send icon spins and the box locks, then the URL changes to `/documents/chat/{sessionId}`. Usually 1 to 3 s.
   - Do not reload or type a URL during this wait. The request text is handed to the chat page in memory only; a reload loses it (the empty chat still exists, so re-type the request in its message box).
8. Wait for the turn to finish. See [Wait for the turn to finish](#wait-for-the-turn-to-finish).
9. Classify the outcome. See [Decide how the turn ended](#decide-how-the-turn-ended).

**Done when:** the URL is `/documents/chat/{sessionId}`, the turn is over and the **Live preview** shows the document.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| "That case is not available to you. The request was not sent." (red, under the box) | The `?case=` value is wrong or not yours | Check the case id with Clarwiz; open `/documents?case={caseId}` again |
| "Could not start a chat. Try again." | Session creation failed | Click **Send** again once; if it repeats, report it |
| Another red message under the box (for example about the client) | Server refused, often a client slug the workspace does not own | Re-pick the client from the list instead of using `?client=` |
| `Not attached — {file}: Only PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF images can be attached` (also "That image is larger than 20MB", "At most 4 images per message") | Image rejected while staging | Remove or replace the file; send other material as a source in the chat |
| Picker still reads **Select a client…** after `?client={clientSlug}` | Slug not in the client list; the binding is still applied and Send will fail | Pick the client from the list instead |

### Wait for the turn to finish
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Preconditions:** you just sent a message, answered blockers, or opened a chat that is still running.

While the assistant works, you will see:
- your message as a right-aligned blue bubble;
- grey italic status lines such as "Thinking...", "Processing...", `Running {n} tools...`, `Working on {Client name}` (the client got bound; the chip at the top of the chat changes from "No client yet" to the client name) and `Attached {file title}`;
- small grey rows with a wrench icon, one per tool call, such as `resolve_client — …`, `get_client_data — …`, `resolve_template — …`, `generate_draft — …`, `edit_sections — …`, `ask_user — …`;
- an assistant bubble (bordered, left-aligned) that streams text and keeps rewriting itself;
- the message box and **Send** disabled, a spinner on **Send**, and the hint next to **Add image** reading "Working...";
- for a new document, the right pane shows a spinner "Drafting your document…" with "It appears here with your details filled in once the assistant finishes." The draft is hidden on purpose until the turn ends. This is not a failure.

**The turn-finished test.** Check every 5 s. The turn is over only when all three are true:
1. The message box (placeholder "Ask for a document, or refine the current draft..."; visual: bottom of the left pane) is enabled.
2. The hint next to **Add image** reads "Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line".
3. The **Send** button shows the paper-plane icon, not a spinner. (Send stays disabled while the box is empty; that is normal.)

Timing: typically 30 s to several minutes. The server stops a turn at 10 minutes. If the connection drops or you reopen the chat mid-turn, a status line reads "Still working on this. The chat updates when the assistant finishes." and the page keeps checking every 3 s for up to 11 minutes; keep waiting. If the turn is still not over after 12 minutes, reload `/documents/chat/{sessionId}` once and apply the test again. If it is still not over, report a failure with the chat URL.

**Done when:** the three conditions hold. Go to [Decide how the turn ended](#decide-how-the-turn-ended).

### Decide how the turn ended
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

Look at the last item in the transcript (left pane), the right pane, and the area above both panes.

| Outcome | What you see | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Finished, draft ready | Right pane shows the rendered document in a frame titled "Document preview". Header shows **Live preview** and a small `v{n}` button (title "Version history"). **PDF (editable)**, **PDF (flat)** and **One page (A4)** are enabled. An **Open in editor** link is visible. The last bubble summarises the draft and may list gaps. | Read the preview and the gap list. Check every fact Clarwiz gave you. Then [Refine the draft](#refine-the-draft) or [Download the PDF](#download-the-pdf). |
| Blocked, structured questions | The last item is a card headed **Blockers** (shown in capitals, with a question-mark icon), one or more questions with a text box or a row of choice buttons, and a **Send answers** button. The preview often still says "No document yet". | [Answer the assistant's questions](#answer-questions-from-the-assistant). |
| Blocked, question in prose | The last assistant bubble asks a question. No Blockers card. The preview may or may not show a draft. | Type the answer in the message box and click **Send**. Wait again. |
| Failed | Red banner at the top of the page, above both panes (texts below). Any draft already made is still shown. | Follow the failure table below. |
| No document, no question | Preview shows "No document yet" and "The draft appears here as soon as the assistant generates it."; the assistant answered in prose (it treated the text as a question). | Send "Draft the document now." and wait again. |

There is no dedicated refusal screen. A refusal arrives as a prose reply with no draft, as the red banner "The assistant could not finish this request.", or for files as a status line `Not attached: {file}. {reason}`.

| If you see (red banner) | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| "The assistant could not finish this request." | The turn failed | Re-send a shorter, simpler request. If a draft exists, ask for the remaining change only. |
| "Still no result from the assistant. Reload this chat to check on it." | The page stopped waiting after 11 minutes | Reload `/documents/chat/{sessionId}` and apply the turn-finished test |
| "Your last message was not sent because the assistant was still busy. Send it again." | Two sends overlapped (often a second tab on the same chat) | Close other tabs on this chat, wait for the turn to finish, send the same text again |
| "The assistant did not reply to your last message. Send it again." | The reply was lost | Send the same text again |
| "This chat is still working on your previous message." | A turn is still running | Wait, then apply the turn-finished test |
| "Session not found" | Wrong chat id, or another user's chat (chats are per user) | Start again from `/documents` |
| "Unable to open that chat" | Load failed | Reload once; if it repeats, start a new chat |
| `Not attached: {file}. Tell the assistant which client this chat is for before attaching a source` | Source file sent to a chat with no client | Tell the assistant the client, wait, then attach the file again in the chat |
| `Not attached: {file}. File must be between 1 byte and 10MB` (or "Unsupported evidence file type…", "Could not extract text…") | File rejected | Use a smaller, supported, text-based file |

### Answer questions from the assistant
**Risk:** `AI` `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** the answers. If Clarwiz did not give you an answer, do not guess: stop and return the exact questions to Clarwiz. Only write "Unknown — leave it blank as a gap" when Clarwiz said to proceed with gaps.

Blockers card:
1. For each question with a row of choice buttons, click the matching choice (button; visual: under the question). The chosen button turns solid.
2. For each question with a text box, click the box directly under the question (input, id `blocker-{questionId}`) and type the answer.
3. Click **Send answers** (button; visual: bottom-right of the card). It stays disabled until every question has an answer.
   - Wait for: the button changes to **Sent**, the inputs lock, an empty blue bubble appears and a new turn starts. Then [Wait for the turn to finish](#wait-for-the-turn-to-finish).

Other cases:
- **None of the choices fits:** do not use the card. Type the answer as a sentence in the message box and click **Send**. The message box is enabled while a Blockers card waits.
- **Card gone after a reload:** Blockers cards are not restored when a chat is reopened. Answer in the message box in plain text.
- **Prose question:** type the answer in the message box (placeholder "Ask for a document, or refine the current draft...") and press Enter or click **Send**.
- **Which client:** usually a Blockers card with client names as choices. Pick the one Clarwiz named. If the chat is already bound to the wrong client, you cannot change it in this chat: click **Switch client (new chat)** (text button; visual: next to the client chip at the top of the chat), which opens `/documents`, and start again with the right client.
- **Which template:** answer with the exact template name (for example "Use the Written Warning template"). If Clarwiz did not say, stop and ask.

**Done when:** the new turn ends with a draft (see the outcome table).

### Refine the draft
**Risk:** `AI` `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Preconditions:** a draft is showing in **Live preview**; the turn is over.

1. Click the message box (placeholder "Ask for a document, or refine the current draft..."; visual: bottom of the left pane).
2. Type all changes in one message, naming each field and value. Example: "Change the incident time to 2:30 pm and add Maria Diaz, front desk, as a second witness."
3. Press Enter or click **Send**.
   - Wait for: the turn to finish (same test). Edits to an existing draft show live in the preview while the turn runs, and the `v{n}` number goes up by one.
4. Read the preview again and check the changed values.

**Done when:** the turn is over, `v{n}` increased and the preview shows the change.

To edit fields by hand instead, click **Open in editor**; see [Document editor](/documents/document-editor).

### Download the PDF
**Risk:** `READ` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Preconditions:** a draft exists (the export buttons are disabled until then); the turn is over.

1. Choose the format in the **Live preview** header (visual: top-right of the right pane):
   - **PDF (editable)** (button, title "Empty fields stay fillable in the downloaded PDF"). Default choice.
   - **PDF (flat)** (button, title "Nothing stays editable in the downloaded PDF"). Use for a final copy.
   - **One page (A4)** (button, title "The whole document scaled onto a single A4 page"). Only for short documents; not available for official PDF forms.
2. Click it once.
   - Wait for: all three buttons read "Preparing…" and a toast reads "Preparing editable PDF…", "Preparing flat PDF…" or "Preparing one-page A4 PDF…". Rendering takes about 5 to 10 s.
3. Confirm the download: a toast reads "editable PDF downloaded", "flat PDF downloaded" or "one-page A4 PDF downloaded", with the file name underneath. The browser saves a file named after the document title.

**Done when:** the success toast shows and the file is in the browser's downloads.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| `This document is too long for one page: it would need to be shrunk to about {n}%, below the {m}% readability limit. Use the regular PDF download.` | One-page export would be unreadable | Use **PDF (editable)** or **PDF (flat)** |
| "One-page export is not available for official PDF forms. Use the regular PDF download." | PDF form document | Use **PDF (editable)** |
| "PDF export runtime is not installed locally." | Server cannot render PDFs | Report it; nothing to fix in the browser |
| `{label} download failed ({status})` | Server error | Retry once; then try the editor's **Share** menu |

Alternative: click **Open in editor**, then **Share** → **Download PDF (editable)**, **Download PDF (not editable)** or **Download one page (A4)**. The editor saves pending edits first; toasts read "Preparing PDF…" then "PDF downloaded". See [Document editor](/documents/document-editor#download-or-print).

### Attach the draft to a case
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Preconditions:** the chat was started from an Operations case (the chat header shows the pill "Case attached — context loads on your first message"); a draft exists.

1. Click **Attach to case** (button with a link icon; visual: in the **Live preview** header, after the PDF buttons). It only appears when the chat has a case and a draft.
   - Wait for: the button reads "Attaching…".
2. Confirm success: toast "Attached to the case — opening it for the reply", and the browser opens `/service-desk?case={caseId}&stage={documentId}` with the draft staged on the case reply.

**Done when:** the URL starts with `/service-desk?case=`. Back in the chat, the button is replaced by the link "Attached to case".

<Warning>**Approval required (EXTERNAL).** Attaching only stages the PDF on the reply. Do not send the case reply from the Operations inbox without explicit human approval.</Warning>

| If you see (red text under the preview header) | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| "This document belongs to a different client than the case." | The chat is bound to another client | Start again from `/documents?case={caseId}` with the case's client |
| "Could not attach the draft to the case. Try again." | Server error | Click **Attach to case** again once |

Alternative from the editor: **Share** → **Attach to case reply** (only listed when the document has an originating case). It is a link to the same `/service-desk?case=…&stage=…` URL.

## Variants

### Variant: a specific client
**Risk:** `WRITE` `AI` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

Bind the client in one of three ways, then continue at step 5 of [Create a document from a request](#create-a-document-from-a-request):
- Open `/documents?client={clientSlug}`. It pre-selects the picker. Use the slug, not the display name.
- Pick it in **Select a client…** (steps 2 to 4).
- Name the client in the request and let the assistant bind it. Watch for the status line `Working on {Client name}` and the chip at the top of the chat. A chat with no client cannot take non-image source files, and the assistant cannot draft until a client is bound.

### Variant: from a template with Use in chat
**Risk:** `WRITE` `AI` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** template name; client; facts.

1. Open `/documents/templates` (or the top tab **Templates** under Documents).
   - Wait for: heading **Templates** and the card grid ("Loading templates…" disappears).
2. Type the template name into the search box (input, aria-label and placeholder "Find a template"; visual: right of the category chips).
3. Click the template card (visual: card with the title, a "Platform" or "Custom" badge and a description). Several templates can share a title; see [Templates](/documents/templates#find-and-preview-a-template).
   - Wait for: a drawer opens on the right with the template title and a preview.
4. Optional: in the drawer, click **Select a client…** and pick the client.
5. Click **Use in chat** (button; visual: drawer footer).
   - Wait for: the URL becomes `/documents` and the request box is pre-filled with `Draft a {Template title} for {Client name}: ` (the client part only if you picked one); the client picker is set.
6. Click at the end of the pre-filled text and type all the facts after the colon. Do not reload: the pre-fill only survives in-app navigation.
7. Click **Send** and continue at step 8 of [Create a document from a request](#create-a-document-from-a-request).

The assistant treats the template as a suggestion. The drawer says: "“Use in chat” describes this template to the assistant, which still picks the template it judges right." If it picks another, answer its question or send `Use the {Template title} template.`

### Variant: from a template with Generate now
**Risk:** `WRITE` (plus `AI` for official PDF forms) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator
**Inputs from Clarwiz:** exact template; client; facts.

Use this when the exact template matters, the assistant is turned off, or the document is an official PDF form. For normal templates no AI writing happens: the new document holds the template's example values plus the client's profile values, and you fill the rest.

1. Do steps 1 to 3 of [Use in chat](#variant-from-a-template-with-use-in-chat).
2. In the drawer, click **Select a client…** and pick the client. Until you do, **Generate now** is disabled with the title "Pick a client to generate straight away".
3. Click **Generate now** (button; visual: drawer footer, next to **Use in chat**).
   - Wait for: a spinner on the button, then the URL changes to `/documents/{id}` (the editor). A few seconds; longer for PDF forms.
4. In the editor, finish the content one of two ways:
   - By hand: if a blue banner reads **Showing template sample data**, click **Clear samples**, then fill each field with the facts. Wait until the toolbar reads "Saved". See [Document editor](/documents/document-editor#edit-fields-and-save).
   - With AI: click the chat icon in the toolbar (icon button, aria-label "Edit this document in chat"). It opens a new chat bound to this document. Send the facts in one message and follow [Wait for the turn to finish](#wait-for-the-turn-to-finish).
5. For an official PDF form (card badge "PDF form · fill-in only"; the drawer says "Fixed PDF form — you can only fill in its fields"): the editor opens the PDF form field editor after one AI extraction pass. Fill or correct fields, then click **Save fields** before downloading. See [Fill an official PDF form](/documents/document-editor#fill-an-official-pdf-form).
6. Download: toolbar **Share** → **Download PDF (editable)**.

**Done when:** the editor reads "Saved" (or **Save fields** reads "Saved" for PDF forms) and the toast "PDF downloaded" shows.

| If you see | Why | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Red text in the drawer body ("Could not generate that document." or a server message) | Generation failed | Retry once; then try another template or the chat path |
| **Generate now** disabled | No client picked | Pick a client in the drawer |

### Variant: from an Operations case
**Risk:** `WRITE` `AI` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

1. Start from the case: in Operations, open the case, and in its Documents pane click **Generate from case**. With the assistant on, this opens `/documents?case={caseId}`. You can also open that URL directly.
   - Wait for: the pill "Case attached — context loads on your first message" under the request box.
2. Follow [Create a document from a request](#create-a-document-from-a-request) from step 2. The case context loads with the first message.
3. When the draft is ready, [Attach the draft to a case](#attach-the-draft-to-a-case).

If the assistant is turned off, **Generate from case** opens the legacy page instead (`/generate?template={slug}&source=service-case&case_id={caseId}`); see [Generate page](/documents/generate-page).

### Variant: from a client profile
**Risk:** `WRITE` (plus `AI` if you use Research + fill) · **Roles:** Principal, Operator

The client page's header button **Generate Document**, the client Documents tab's **Generate** or **Generate a document**, the Roadmap tab's **Draft** or **Draft deliverable**, and **Draft with context** all open the legacy page `/generate?client={clientSlug}` (with extra parameters). Short version:

1. Wait for the template name as the middle-column heading and the preview status "Ready".
2. Pick the template in the left list if it is not already selected.
3. Check the **Client** select shows the right client (it can auto-pick the first client).
4. Fill the form; optionally click **Research + fill**, then **Apply**.
5. Click **Generate** (top-right of the page header). Wait for the toast `Generated "{title}"` and the redirect to `/documents/{id}`.
6. In the editor: **Share** → **Download PDF (editable)**.

Full recipe and every query parameter: [Generate page](/documents/generate-page).

### Variant: packet run
**Risk:** `WRITE` · **Roles:** Principal, Operator (both can run; only Principal and Admin can create or edit packets)

1. Open `/documents/packets` (or top tab **Packets** under Documents).
2. On the packet's row click **Run**.
3. In the panel that opens, click **Select a client…** and pick the client.
4. Click `Generate {n} documents`.
   - Wait for: the spinner to stop; a result line `{created} created` (plus `, {failed} failed`) and one row per template with **Open** or a red error.
5. Open each result with a middle-click or in a new tab. The result list is not kept: leaving the page loses it. Later, find the documents on `/documents` (newest first; search by client slug).
6. In each editor: fill the remaining fields, then **Share** → **Download PDF (editable)**.

Packet documents hold template defaults plus client data, with no AI-written prose. Full recipe: [Brand and packets](/documents/brand-and-packets#run-a-packet-for-one-client).

## Waits and signals

| Moment | Signal while waiting | Finished when | Typical time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landing loads | "Loading your documents…" | Heading **What do you need?** and the list | 1 to 3 s |
| Landing **Send** | Send icon spins, box locked | URL is `/documents/chat/{sessionId}` | 1 to 3 s |
| Chat turn | "Working...", status lines, wrench rows, streaming bubble, "Drafting your document…" | The turn-finished test passes | 30 s to several minutes; 10 min hard limit |
| Reopened mid-turn | "Still working on this. The chat updates when the assistant finishes." | Same test; the page re-checks every 3 s for up to 11 min | as above |
| PDF download | "Preparing…" on the buttons, loading toast | Toast `… PDF downloaded` | 5 to 10 s |
| Attach to case | "Attaching…" | Toast "Attached to the case — opening it for the reply" and new URL | 1 to 3 s |
| Generate now | Spinner on **Generate now** | URL `/documents/{id}` | a few seconds |

## Gotchas

- Do not reload between landing **Send** and the chat page appearing; the opening request is lost (the empty chat remains).
- The message box is locked for the whole turn. You cannot queue a second message.
- Use one browser tab per chat. A second tab sending to the same chat produces "Your last message was not sent because the assistant was still busy. Send it again."
- A new document stays hidden behind "Drafting your document…" until the turn ends.
- Blockers cards disappear after a reload; answer in the message box instead.
- The client cannot be changed inside a chat. Use **Switch client (new chat)** and start over.
- The landing client picker cannot be cleared; reload `/documents` to reset it.
- The assistant always needs a client before it can draft, and it cannot create the three official PDF forms. Use [Generate now](#variant-from-a-template-with-generate-now) for those.
- The assistant may write to the client profile on its own while it works (it approves its own tool calls).
- The preview is a sandboxed frame. If your tooling cannot read text inside it, use a screenshot, or click **Open in editor**, where every value is a form field.
- Google Docs export and e-signature are not available anywhere in Documents (the Google Docs buttons are not rendered; **E-signature delivery** is disabled).
